# P15.7 notes — the resident readings

**The instrument is landed and `gzip-resident` is registered untaken**
(`measure.UNTAKEN`). Its readings are the next round's. A figure is taken from
a commit, never from a tree carrying its own apparatus
([`measurements.md`](measurements.md), "A figure may be published outside the
sweep"), so the readings are `cd scripts && uv run measure.py --figure
gzip-resident` at the commit carrying this. That is 36 runs of about ten
seconds, plus `control_bgzf`'s first generation (about four minutes) and the
two builds, so no handoff is needed. The row's split is in
[`../status/history/2026-10-25.md`](../status/history/2026-10-25.md), "15.7
split: three reviews".

- **Two legs, one a charge.** A single member's first `parse` is its first
  pass, billed per worker with no pool (`measure.gzip_first_pass_charge`). A
  BGZF file is walked at open, so its first `parse` block-decodes and is
  billed as `.xz` is, with gzip's decoder (`measure.gzip_block_charge`). Each
  runs at `--jobs` 1, 4 and 24 under `parallel-peak-rss`'s stated allowance
  and container, with gate twins. Every cell is asked `reserve`'s three
  lines with its own bill: `charge_fault`, split out of
  `charge_model_problem`, and `charge_verdict`, split out of `run_reserve`
  with no change to what `reserve` renders.
- **`control_bgzf` is new**: `generate_gzip_input.py --bgzf` writes
  `control` as BGZF through the same zlib and level as `control_gz`, a member
  every 65,280 bytes as `bgzip` writes, with `bgzip` itself unpinned (the
  spec's "Fixtures"). An oversized member is refused rather than split. The
  generator's file is shared, so `inflate-throughput`, `gzseek-backend` and
  `gzseek-spacing` go stale on it, and `control_gz` is regenerated by stamp.
  Its bytes come from code this change leaves alone, so nothing re-takes
  those figures.
- **The bill is mirrored and then held to the run's own**: each run's `scan
  started` budget must equal the mirror, and its count must equal the row's,
  or the figure refuses. A first-pass leg must open a first pass and a BGZF
  leg must not (`measure.parse_first_pass` reads the started line's
  `workers=`). Trial runs of a dev build, by hand, stated exactly the six
  bills `GzipResident.STATED_BILLS` holds. In those runs every first pass ran
  as many workers as its row's count, and the BGZF walk found 128 units of
  386 members.
- **What would falsify the hypothesis** is written in
  `measure.run_gzip_resident`'s docstring, ahead of any reading. The
  hypothesis is that `MEMORY_UNPOOLED_BOUND`, read off `.xz` alone, bounds
  what gzip holds outside its charge.

## What the readings round inherits

- **The secant prices the speculation limit**: what each worker between 4
  and 24 held, against `Chunking::task_bytes` on the first-pass leg, four
  speculation limits of it. It reopens the limit only where the charge
  rather than the cores bounds the workers at a memory a user runs
  ([`../status/history/2026-10-24.md`](../status/history/2026-10-24.md),
  "15.8.1's chunking, reviewed"). A cell over the bound also reopens the
  spacing beside it ([`decisions.md`](decisions.md), "D113").
- **A serial first pass is billed a task it never runs**:
  `default_worker_memory` charges the larger of the pass and a task on any
  file of more than one chunk, whatever the count. Expect the one-job
  first-pass cell's remainder to sit low. That is not an over-bill unless the
  remainder goes negative.
- **Taking it moves the figure into `FIGURES`**, and the section goes in
  `measurements.md` with its marker, as `gzseek-spacing`'s did.

## Negative results

- **No leg varies the limit or the chunk length**: the chunk-length legs are
  15.7.1's throughput figure, and the limit is priced only by this figure's
  secant (the spec's "Slice order").
- **No fetched leg**: no figure reads a remote first pass, and the download a
  fetched pass adds to each worker's charge is unmeasured here.

## `M295`: a range's bounds compare under its own collation

- **`TypeKind::Range` carries `collation`**, the `collation = <collation>` parameter verbatim, and `range_comparison`
  asks the bound under it as a column's `COLLATE` clause, for the range and its multirange companion alike; a range
  stating none reaches its domain subtype's clause, or the no-clause arm, as before. I95 files what `pg_dump` writes and
  what the server compares under; I46's scope limit points at it. `CACHE_FORMAT_VERSION` is 88, `PERSISTED_INDEX`
  re-pinned (`public.textrange`'s parameter now persists), `GOLDEN_ORDER`'s digest unmoved. `emitter_register.py`'s
  `READS` holds `collation` as a clause of `CREATE TYPE`. `KD144` is struck with its marker.
- **The tests**: `ddl/types/tests.rs`'s `parses_a_range_type_stating_a_collation`, and `compare/tests.rs`'s
  `a_range_compares_its_bounds_under_its_own_collation`, which the parent fails at its first case, `C` diverging as
  `UnknownCollation`; `tests/preamble.rs` reads the fixtures' `public.textrange` as stating `pg_catalog."C"`. A
  registration of the `types` fixture no longer names `public.t_text_range.v_textrange` as compared bytewise.
- **`pgdt info` does not print the parameter**, a domain's `COLLATE` being printed and a range's not; the row named
  no display.

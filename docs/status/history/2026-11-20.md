## `M296`: a kept forward decode is left once its unit closes

- **`Pass::route` continues a kept decode behind the frontier only where it serves in the unit it stands in**
  (`Behind::serves_in`): a closed unit's decode always, a forward one only while its unit is the open one. Once the
  frontier has closed that unit, the read begins the closed unit's decode at its checkpoint, compared against the
  CRC-32 the unit recorded. Bytes the forward decode handed out before the unit closed stay the frontier's to check.
  `KD137` is struck with its marker; `complete_reads`' and `FirstPass`'s rustdoc lose its exception.
- **The test**: `first_pass/tests.rs`'s `a_kept_forward_decode_is_left_once_its_unit_closes`, on both feeds, which the
  parent's `route` fails at "once it has closed", the kept decode still the forward one.

## `M25`: the sweep pair, and why the doc carries the noisier of the two

Two full `scripts/measure.py --all` sweeps ran detached against `4c2c3e7`,
back to back with a 120 s settle — `runs/measure-20260830T025850` and
`runs/measure-20260830T032507`, both exit 0, 48 readings each, no figure
failed and neither `tables.md` was marked unpublishable. Sweep 1 is what
`measurements.md` now carries; `session-drift` is derived across the pair.

**The pair is about two minutes apart, where the published pair was two hours**,
and that turns out to be a term in the figure rather than a detail of how it was
taken: median absolute drift is **0.4%** here against **5.6%** there, largest
7.3% against 8.5%. The cold `INSERT` scan is the clearest case — it drifted
8.1% at two hours and 0.3% at two minutes, so the reading that was read as
"a cold scan with real CPU in it drifts like a warm one" is really "…given
enough elapsed time". The two-hour number stays the one to plan against, since
that is what comparing across sessions actually costs.

**Neither sweep is clean on both witnesses, and they fail different ones.** The
standing gate is the co-measured warm `dd` floor, because bandwidth contention
is invisible to every counter. Judged on it:

- Sweep 1's floor is flat — 0.311 s on the control file against the doc's
  0.309, 0.312 on the arrays file against 0.314. But its last two figures ran
  under a CPU episode the counters *did* see: ≤12% and ≤9% machine-busy against
  ≤5% everywhere else in both sweeps, under the armed gate's 15 so the harness
  published it, with `map-only`'s three readings 4–7% high and their reps
  spread 7–11%.
- Sweep 2 never exceeded 4.7% machine-busy — tighter than the sweep behind the
  previous stamp — and its reps are tight throughout. But its warm `control`
  floor read 0.291–0.294 s against the doc's 0.309–0.310, a 5.5% move, and the
  readings on that file moved with it (−3.3% on the `COPY` scan, −5.8% on the
  census-off leg) while the arrays file's floor and readings barely moved. A
  per-file effect on tmpfs, not a machine-wide one.

The gate was applied as written, so sweep 1 is published and the call is filed
under `STATUS.md`'s "Decisions worth another look" with the argument for the
other choice: contention makes a floor *slower*, sweep 2's is faster, and 5.5%
sits inside the drift envelope — the two sweeps behind the `fa186ab` stamp
differed by 4.3% on that same floor and both were published. Both `raw.json`
files are kept, so reversing it costs no measuring.

**What moved past drift.** Most figures reproduce the previous stamp within
2%. The exceptions worth knowing: the census on brace-free rows reads +8%
(0.038 s, 47 ns/row) against +7%, both inside the range earlier sweeps read;
the `INSERT`-to-`COPY` per-byte ratio reads 14.4× against 14.6×, and the four
witnessed-quiet sweeps now span 14.4–14.9×; the 601-byte `String::from` control
in the nested micro read 42 and 41 ns in this pair against 24 ns before, which
alone swings that row's `÷ copy` from 221× to 122× while `decode` moves 2% —
so that column is now written up as an order of magnitude rather than a figure.

**The per-block quadratic's derived split no longer resolves.** `map-only`
minus the throttled `parse` at 4000 blocks is the cache's share, and it is the
difference of two ~21 s readings taken in different figures: 0.76 s at the
previous stamp, 0.17 s in sweep 1, 1.41 s in sweep 2. The doc now says "under a
second" and says why, rather than quoting a number the instrument cannot
resolve.

## What the committing session owes

The fold-in was left uncommitted. It edits `scripts/measure.py` — emptying
`ACKNOWLEDGED` of the two entries the new stamp spent, and two `section=`
strings that track headings this sweep moved — and `measure.py` is what
`session-drift` declares, so `uv run measure.py --stale` reads that figure red
again the moment the fold-in lands. That is the mechanism working: an
uncommitted path is never excused. **Once the commit exists, acknowledge it for
`session-drift`** — the change is confined to the acknowledgement register and
two display strings, so no measured path differs, which makes it the same
weaker kind of entry `5a207de` was: a read diff with nothing mechanical behind
it.

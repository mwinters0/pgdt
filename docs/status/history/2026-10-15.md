## P36.4's two calls reviewed: the gatherer stands, `compare` earns 36.4.1

The gatherer keys each field by its set's kind, not from the plan. That call
stands, and D79's Rejected line says why.

`ValueKey::compare` answered `Some` for keys no order relates (`KD118`). The
spec's "Keys of different kinds never compare" is amended to "Keys no order
relates never compare". The closure is 36.4.1: a variant per order, `Decimal`
carrying its scale, and the specials their domain. Two other designs were
rejected:
- **A kind tag checked by `compare`** is too strict, since `varchar(5)`
  against `text` is ordered alike. It is also costly: an enum kind carries its
  label list.
- **Calling cross-plan answers unspecified** leaves an API returning `Some`
  where it is wrong.

The split rides the variant match `order_keys` already makes, as `Interval`
and `IntervalFields` do. It is a hot-path change, so it is counted on its own
commit. `ValueKey` is not on the embedder surface (`roadmap.md`, "Pre-1.0: no
compatibility obligations").

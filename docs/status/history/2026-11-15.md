## The floor takes off what is resident or reserved beside the line: 33.30

Settled with the maintainer, reviewing STATUS's entry that the floor leaves out what is `held` (33.5, D161).

- **`held` is three kinds of thing, and only one refuses every long line.** In the provider it is
  `maps + pool + lines` (`ScanBudget::draw`); in `pgdt query`'s replay the settled table's statistics
  (`statistics_held`). The pool grant is a limit on DataFusion's operators, not a holding, and may pass the allowance
  (`pgdt-sql.md`'s 8 GiB grant in 4 GiB); statistics are resident for the run; other live scans' line charges are
  reservations, resident when those lines are held.
- **The floor already spends the margin**: one reader holding a line at it predicts resident at the allowance less
  128 MiB (`MEMORY_RESERVE − MEMORY_UNPOOLED_BOUND`) plus whatever `held` is, so past that slack an ignored holding
  is the OOM kill the floor exists to turn into a message. D83's "off the ceiling alone" read onto the floor is
  `max(cap, ceiling − held)`, today's floor above the 640 MiB crossover: it sizes a count, where the floor decides a
  refusal.
- **The floor already depends on what else the session runs**, through `drawn`; it leaves out those same scans' line
  charges, so a join's two scans each admit a line past its room up to a floor ignoring the other's — about twice the
  cap between two ledgers.
- **Settled**: the pool grant stays off the floor (D161's rejection, its reason narrowed to the grant); other live
  scans' line charges come off it, as their budgets do; the statistics the run keeps resident come off it — a cache
  grown by a back-fill holds more and refuses more, deterministic per cache. The spec's "The floor is one reader" is
  amended. D161, `one_reader_leaves` and `ArrangementRequest` keep describing what is built until 33.30 lands.
- **What comes off is what stays beside the line as the run is carved.** A mapping pass's statistics grow after it
  (the account's total replacing `held` in `Ledger::draw_statistics`), bounded by `statistics_allowance`; that growth
  stays off the floor, the floor a number the arrangement fixes and statistics first (D157). Refused: all they may
  hold off it, about the room, a fifth of the allowance left above 640 MiB; statistics yielding to a line past the
  room, which makes the cache depend on when a line was held; what they hold at the draw, a refusal by timing.
- **One rule under an estimate too**: `pgdt`'s uncarved half-available takes the same holdings off, as it does
  `drawn`; D11's "nothing drawn or held changes it" sizes the pools, where the floor claims the line fits.
- **The refusal names what came off**: `Floor` carries the bytes beside the line and `LineOutgrowsMemory` names them
  beside `holds` and the allowance; the remedy stays the allowance, the shell's other lever, running fewer scans,
  being `KD38`'s.
- **Routed**: **33.30** admitted after 33.5's rows — `ArrangementRequest` telling the three apart, the line charges
  still off the ceiling alone for the count (D160), the floor taking off what is resident or reserved.

# 2026-09-15

## P20 is grilled and sliced

The spec is [`roadmap-P20-statistics-memory.md`](../../design/roadmap-P20-statistics-memory.md);
the checklist is [`../STATUS.md`](../STATUS.md), "P20 progress", and its inbox
is drained and deleted. **Pick up at 20.1**, the statistics account, which
lands before anything declines against it.

**What changed the plan.**

- **Statistics grow with a dump's length, not only its width**: finished
  groups stay resident until exit, so under the shipped defaults koji alone
  would hold on the order of 1.6 GiB of them. That moved length into
  "reasonable data" and put a per-block group cap in the phase. The estimate's
  source is `runs/pgdq-koji-20260905-0054/final-info.log`; the arithmetic is
  the spec's "Facts it rests on".
- **A new standing rule**, [`roadmap.md`](../../design/roadmap.md), "Two
  tunables fit pgdq to hardware: memory and parallelism". It renames
  `--parallel-memory` to `--memory`, meaning resident, and its allowlist check
  is built in 20.3.
- **One reserve for every operation, its size measured in 20.7.** Slicing
  first set 512 MiB as a judgement "confirmed" by re-taking `reserve`; that
  confirmation could not fail — the criterion faults only a reserve too small,
  384 MiB already met it, and those legs neither gather nor query — so the
  maintainer directed the reserve be measured. The instrument, legs, criterion
  and the bill-if-it-grows branch are registered in the spec before the
  sitting ("The reserve rises uniformly, and is measured"); the constant stays
  384 MiB until then.
- **Every koji cache in `runs/` is unreadable by the current build** (`KD30`),
  so 20.6's `query` over a gathered cache needs the gathering run first.

## P20 reopened: granularity follows row density

**Pick up at 20.1, then 20.8**, koji's row-density reading, which runs detached
before any granularity code. The decisions are
[`roadmap-P20-statistics-memory.md`](../../design/roadmap-P20-statistics-memory.md),
"Granularity follows row density", and the re-cut checklist is
[`../STATUS.md`](../STATUS.md), "P20 progress"; no slice has landed.

**What changed the plan.** A uniform `2^20` group costs an entry per column
however few rows it holds, so a table of wide rows carries statistics per row
that a table of narrow ones does not. The maintainer directed that a block's
group size coarsen by powers of two where rows are sparse, decided from the
whole block's row-density distribution within the mapping pass, and folded
into P20 rather than run ahead of it: coarsening is the cap's merge, and
20.1's account prices it. The facts it rests on are the spec's "Facts it rests
on".

**Admitted**: 20.8 (the reading), 20.9 (the density minimum) and 20.10 (a stated
maximum). Sizing a fixed-width table from its header is a Future item in
[`roadmap.md`](../../design/roadmap.md), "Future — wanted, unscheduled".

## The ledger lends under the margin's ceiling; the cap is the floor's

Settled with the maintainer, reviewing STATUS's entry that 33.4 carves the ledger from the cap.

- **Above 640 MiB the ledger as landed is the margin itself.** The cap (`allowance − MEMORY_RESERVE`) and the
  margin's ceiling (`margin_allowance`) cross at `5 × (MEMORY_RESERVE − MEMORY_UNPOOLED_BOUND)`; above it the readers'
  count is solved against the ceiling (`Parallelism::within_shared`), so `cap − budget` lies almost wholly above it,
  about a fifth of the allowance less 128 MiB, and the bytes the entry named — those statistics and the ledger both
  lend — are only one worker's step of slack. A full ledger predicts resident at the allowance less 128 MiB, against
  KD34's attributed excess.
- **A held line is a charge, bounded by the margin as a reader's pool and the statistics are**: the ledger lends
  what the ceiling leaves the budget, so above the crossover a long line parks readers, as the spec's "the carve
  fills its cap with readers, leaving the ledger little" expects. **The cap is reached only at the floor**: down to
  one reader, the earliest line may take what the cap leaves `at(1)`, as `Parallelism::fit` budgets one worker at
  whatever the cap is while the margin bounds the count. The spec's "The floor is one reader" stands unamended.
- **Statistics and held lines share that one room, statistics first.** The statistics account charges the ledger as a
  holder that never waits and is never declined for a line; lines are admitted from what it leaves, and a charge passing
  the room asks the latest holders to give up. A block's statistics then never depend on when a long line was held — a
  decline loses them from the cache for good, where a line given up costs a re-read — and a query's replay, gathering
  none, lends its lines the room less the settled statistics it keeps resident. Refused: both claiming the room, which
  passes the ceiling by it; a fixed split, which nothing measured sizes and a query leaves idle; first come, which makes
  the cache vary run to run.
- **D157's rejection of carving under the ceiling is struck**: it read the floor's `at(1)` against the cap as the
  ledger's steady-state capacity. D157 keeps describing the cap's carve until the work below lands.
- **Routed**: **33.4.4** admitted before 33.4.1 — the re-carve, wherever an allowance is found, an estimate's included,
  the longest line's charge inside the room, and the statistics' draw, tested on its own; 33.4.1 gains the account's
  charging it; 33.5 gains the earliest line at one reader admitted up to what the cap leaves `at(1)`.

## `KD168` struck: a `jsonb` number compared is keyed whole

`KD168` read a compared number's copies as a ratio the key factor misstates.
They are a constant: a number is keyed whole from its text, bounded by
numeric's precision (`numeric_stores`) on a short line as on a held one, and a
dump's field writes no exponent to pad (I41's output bullet). The ledger
drawing the factor therefore charged nothing short. Comparing in place is
rejected in D152; the property is at `JSONB_KEY_FACTOR`, uncharged as a
short value's decode is.

## The replay takes its arrangement whole, in 33.4.3

`TablePartitions::drawing_on` pairs a plan's ledger with whatever parallelism
its `QueryOptions` carry, where every mapping pass takes `&Arrangement`, which
is the spec's "Where it lives". 33.4.3 must know a query's factor before
resolving its arrangement, today `ReplayPlan::factor` computed beside the cut
that reads the parallelism, so its order is options, line charge, arrangement,
plan. Its row now hands the plan the arrangement whole: `Table::plan` taking
`&Arrangement`, `QueryOptions` losing `parallelism`, the builder gone, so no
ledger meets a parallelism not carved beside it (D83, D157).

## A live provider scan's line charge is billed to later scans: 33.4.5

Settled with the maintainer, reviewing 33.4.3's provider carve (D160).
`ScanBudget::draw` adds a scan's read-buffer budget to `held.scans` and
nothing for the `line_charge` it carved, so a scan drawn while it lives solves
its count against a ceiling still holding that line's room, and two ledgers
lend the same bytes: a join's resident can pass the session's one number.
Each live scan's charge is billed to every later draw as `held`, off the
margin's ceiling alone (D157's "never off a carved cap"). Refused: one ledger
for the session, against the spec's ledger per scan, its gain over the charge
being the slack a carved first scan leaves, which is `KD38`'s. **33.4.5**
admitted before 33.5, whose floor reads the same room.

## The charge counts only lines the read can hold: 33.4.6

Settled with the maintainer, reviewing D160. `LongestLine` holds its offset and
a pruned run is a byte range (`ReplayPlan::segments`), so the record places a
line; what it cannot bound is a kept run's own longest, so the one exact
exclusion is a block whose every run the static filter prunes. A read whose
batch, static filter and sorted stop read no field streams every line (the
spec's "A pass holds a line only where it decodes it", `reads_no_field`, D138),
yet D160 charges it, which 33.5's floor would turn into refusing a `count(*)`
the spec calls flat. The dynamic filter D160 cites enters after the draw
(`TablePartitions::under`, D95) and may keep any row, so such a plan streams
and keeps a long row whatever it reads. D160's rejection of both is struck, by
**33.4.6**, admitted before 33.5 and landed. A
longest line per row group, bounding a kept run exactly, waits on a figure.

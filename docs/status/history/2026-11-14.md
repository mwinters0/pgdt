## The ledger lends under the margin's ceiling; the cap is the floor's

Settled with the maintainer, reviewing STATUS's entry that 33.4 carves the ledger from the cap.

- **Above 640 MiB the ledger as landed is the margin itself.** The cap (`allowance − MEMORY_RESERVE`) and the
  margin's ceiling (`margin_allowance`) cross at `5 × (MEMORY_RESERVE − MEMORY_UNPOOLED_BOUND)`; above it the readers'
  count is solved against the ceiling (`Parallelism::within_shared`), so `cap − budget` lies almost wholly above it,
  about a fifth of the allowance less 128 MiB, and the bytes the entry named — those statistics and the ledger both
  lend — are only one worker's step of slack. A full ledger predicts resident at the allowance less 128 MiB, against
  KD34's attributed excess.
- **A held line is a charge, bounded by the margin as a reader's pool and the statistics are**: the ledger lends
  what the ceiling leaves the budget, so above the crossover a long line parks readers, as the spec's "the carve
  fills its cap with readers, leaving the ledger little" expects. **The cap is reached only at the floor**: down to
  one reader, the earliest line may take what the cap leaves `at(1)`, as `Parallelism::fit` budgets one worker at
  whatever the cap is while the margin bounds the count. The spec's "The floor is one reader" stands unamended.
- **Statistics and held lines share that one room, statistics first.** The statistics account charges the ledger as
  a holder that never waits and is never declined for a line; lines are admitted from what it leaves, and a charge
  passing the room asks the latest holders to give up. A block's statistics then never depend on when a long line was
  held — a decline loses them from the cache for good, where a line given up costs a re-read — and a query's replay,
  gathering none, lends its lines the whole room. Refused: both claiming the room, which passes the ceiling by it; a
  fixed split, which nothing measured sizes and a query leaves idle; first come, which makes the cache vary run to run.
- **D157's rejection of carving under the ceiling is struck**: it read the floor's `at(1)` against the cap as the
  ledger's steady-state capacity. D157 keeps describing the cap's carve until the work below lands.
- **Routed**: **33.4.4** admitted before 33.4.1 — the re-carve, wherever an allowance is found, an estimate's included,
  the longest line's charge inside the room, and the statistics' draw, tested on its own; 33.4.1 gains the account's
  charging it; 33.5 gains the earliest line at one reader admitted up to what the cap leaves `at(1)`.

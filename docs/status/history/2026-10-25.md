# 2026-10-25

## 15.8.3's header read, reviewed

The call under STATUS's "Decisions worth another look" — a speculation reading
a BGZF chunk off its headers wherever it finds one, not only on a fetched BGZF
file — was put to the maintainer and **affirmed**; the review found the chain
read trusting a length its module doc said was borne out, and earned 15.8.5.

- **The file decides, chunk by chunk.** The reasoning and the two flags
  refused are D117's "Rejected"; its correctness never rests on the guess, the
  frontier proving every start (`../../design/decisions.md`, "D117").
- **The scan for a header is not priced apart, nor sped up**: 15.7 prices the
  pass as built, and D117's "Reopens" names the profile that would reopen it.
- **A member length reaching the chunk's end is taken as stated.**
  `Members::read` (`crates/gz-seek/src/speculate/members.rs`) reads the header
  after a member only where that member ends inside the chunk, so a lone first
  header within a member's length of the chunk's end, and every chain's last
  member, are trusted on `BSIZE` and an `ISIZE` under 64 KiB. A wrong one costs
  the chain of windows at that chunk, never the index. The module doc, D117 and
  15.8.3's notes said every link was borne out; they now say what is built.
- **15.8.5 bears every length out**, in `gz-seek` alone: a link is taken only
  where a BGZF header, a plain member's magic or the file's end follows it, a
  first header nothing bears out being searched past, so a false or lone
  header falls back to the native search instead of handing out a guess never
  taken. It earns a third level as 15.8.4 did, follows 15.8.3 and precedes
  15.7; 15.16 does not wait on it.

## 15.10's static-block unit end, reviewed

The call under STATUS's "Decisions worth another look" — a unit ending inside
a static block held to its plaintext length and CRC-32, its compressed end
unchecked — was put to the maintainer and **affirmed**; nothing in the
mechanism changed. The reasoning is D118's (`../../design/decisions.md`,
"D118").

- **A checkpoint at the wrong bit is still refused, one unit later.** Every
  unit not of whole members carries a CRC-32 (`Index::from_parts`,
  `crates/gz-seek/src/index.rs`), and the unit a `Start::Symbol` checkpoint
  begins resumes at its bit; the unit ending there has handed out right
  plaintext. Holding the bit would only name the refusal sooner, and `pgdt-io`
  tells no `gz_seek` error from another but `InvalidIndex`.
- **Nothing needs the compressed end**: `compressed_range()` ends at the byte
  holding the unit's last bit, and every unit decodes from exactly it, static
  blocks included (`crates/gz-seek/src/forward/tests.rs`).
- **The free check was refused too**: a lower bound on the bytes the inflater
  consumed, which every decoder obeys, catches only a misplacement past its
  lookahead, and an upper bound rests on each engine's undocumented lookahead.
- **No test pins the refusal**, the misplacement tests moving block-boundary
  ends only; `M249` adds it.

## 15.7 split: three reviews

15.7 was one row covering three reviews, each needing its own apparatus or
none. One is an instrument against the charge model. One is a throughput
instrument whose chunk-length legs need a way to vary `Chunking::SHIPPED`,
and whose writers' files need generators of their own. The last is the
manual, which writes from what the readings find
(`../../process.md`, "Slice numbering").

- **15.7 is the resident readings**, its row rewritten to that scope. The
  instrument is `gzip-resident`, registered untaken; the readings and what
  they reopen are the next round's
  (`../../design/roadmap-P15.7-resident-notes.md`).
- **15.7.1 is the throughput readings**: the gzip throughput figures, and the
  parallel first pass on more than one writer's files and at two chunk
  lengths (the spec's "Slice order").
- **15.7.2 is the record**: the manual and `pg-dump-compatibility.md`. It
  comes last because the manual says which writers' files the parallel first
  pass reaches, and that is 15.7.1's reading. The spec is unchanged.

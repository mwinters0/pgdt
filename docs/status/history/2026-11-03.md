## `M277`: a refusal quotes its field as the restore does

`FieldRefusal::value` is the field's text as the restore's error quotes it (I94): whole to 100 bytes, past them its
longest head of whole characters within 100 and `...`, read off the field's head by `pgdt-core`'s `printout_head`
without unescaping the rest. Both observers making one — the gatherer's (`gatherer.rs`) and the strict check's
(`check_row`) — quote by it, so `Error::FieldRefused`, `ColumnRefusals::first` and `info`'s line beside each block quote
alike; `CACHE_FORMAT_VERSION` is bumped for the shorter record. I94's quote was observed on postgres 13.23, 16.15 and
18.6. Accepted by `copy.rs`'s `a_field_is_quoted_by_its_head_as_a_restore_quotes_it` and `tests/statistics.rs`'s
`a_refused_field_is_quoted_by_its_head_as_a_restore_quotes_it`, a default, a strict and an ignoring parse's record each
quoting one field of 301 bytes written escaped.

A refused walked value (D143) is still unescaped whole once, by the `field_refused` call it falls through to in
`check_row`; that and every other scalar's whole read are 33.13.3's and 33.19's.

## `resumable-reader-micro`'s noise and verdict, settled with the maintainer

Closed from STATUS's "Decisions worth another look". The figure is untaken, so both changes land in 33.17's instrument
before any reading; its row says what remains.

- **The noise gains a layout term.** 2026-11-02's settlement named `nested-decode-micro`'s noise, for which the record
  holds no number, its stamps mixing code moves with drift. 33.17 built the sitting's own scatter
  (`measure.ResumableRow.noise_ns`), which cannot see code layout: the three forms are separate functions of one
  binary, and `measurements.md`, "Two builds of one source can differ by layout", records a move of that kind in a
  byte-at-a-time loop. The bench runs a second time built with `-C llvm-args=-align-all-functions=6`, in a target dir
  of its own as the allocator legs are, and each row's noise widens by how far `async − pull` moves between the two
  builds; the gaps are read off the shipped build.
- **`push` is taken only where it pays.** `pull` is the bar, not a candidate, resuming at no run. `push` is taken only
  if, on every one-run row where `async` is past its noise of `pull`, `push` is cheaper than `async` past their own
  noise, formed as `async`'s against `pull` is and widened by the layout term of `push − async`; otherwise `async`,
  the verdict saying neither form holds today's price. Today's `resumable_verdict` reads no `push` column.
- **Only the one-run rows decide**, affirmed and filed at `resumable_verdict`; what that leaves 33.18 is in 33.17's
  notes, "What the readings round inherits".

## `M278`: a query's decode error quotes its field's head

`Error::FieldDecode` carries its field whole: the batch's append path takes `append_typed`'s error, the text itself
(`batch/builder.rs`, `fail`), and a filter term's `field_decode` (`predicate/eval.rs`) copies its decoded value. So on a
long field the message is the field, and the copy is made while the line and its decoded text are still held, a third
copy the spec's charge does not count (`roadmap-P33-line-bound.md`, "What a held line costs"). `M277` clipped
`FieldRefused` and `ColumnRefusals::first` only. Admitted: both quote by `FieldRefused`'s rule (I94), one rule for
every field an error names, though a decode error is no restore's. It blocks P33, landing ahead of its next slice: the spec's charge holds on
that path only once it lands, and no slice's testing should meet a decode error copying its field.

## `M242`: a block in flight is decoded once

- **Admitted with the maintainer, out-of-band**: `KD20` was `(c) unowned`, P23
  only sketched, and the refusal it carried — a second lock in front of every
  miss — was a marker comment, not a `D<k>`. The decode-in-flight list now
  shares the retention list's mutex (`crates/pgdt-io/src/source/xz/blocks.rs`,
  `Retention`), so a miss that collides with nobody takes one lock as before.
  A waiter that finds the flight empty (failed decode, dropped fetched future)
  claims again; errors are not shared.
- **No figure has read it.** `parallel-scan-throughput` and
  `reserve --alone` are owed; `KD111`'s fate is the second's

  (`runs/m242-retake-20261021-132517.log`) yielded nothing: the first figure
  failed in its untimed builder, and the second was stopped mid-reps when other
  work took the machine.
- **Re-takes relaunched at `de2f6076`, detached and unread past their first
  timed reading**: the same two figures, in the same order, logged to
  `runs/m243-retake-20261021-144609.log` (process group 3051769), the first
  writing `runs/measure-20261021T144610/`. `mount_preflight` passed and the

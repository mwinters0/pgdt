## P33 re-targeted: a parse streams the types a dump's long fields hold

A review of P33's arc with the maintainer found the 2026-11-02 re-grill had set a target of a parse holding nothing
that grows with any field of any type, in any spelling `*_in` reads. That is a codec whose readers all stream, which
the spec as first specified rejected on exactly that ground. Every scalar family is a port of its input function with
its own quirks, sized only once touched, so each slice split (2026-11-08, 2026-11-09). Meanwhile 33.4–33.7, the ledger
and the knob's removal, waited behind every reader. The facts that settled it:

- The reference dump's long lines are one `text` field (2026-11-02's entry), which streams under either target.
- PostgreSQL holds a line whole to restore it (`copyfromparse.c`, `line_buf`, then `attribute_buf`).
- 33.13.4's and 33.13.5's push machines cost the hot path. From `d33fa096` to `f799618c`, `instructions:u` rose on
  the data-level parse, the typed query and `pgdt sql -c` (`runs/count-pair-33.22-20261109/log-33.13.4`,
  `runs/count-pair-33.22-rework-20261109/pair-33.13.5/log`), placed in the machines
  ([`roadmap-P33.22-priced-notes.md`](../../design/roadmap-P33.22-priced-notes.md)).

Settled, in the spec's "A pass holds a line only where it decodes it" and STATUS's slices:

- **A parse streams `text`, `character`, `bytea`, `json`, `jsonb`, the walked containers, and 33.13.3's strict-only
  readers.** A scalar that a key or a query's batch reads is collected whole by its slice reader and drawn from the
  ledger (33.4). The acceptance stands: a strict parse under a `--memory` below the reference dump's longest line.
- **33.13.4 and 33.13.5 are reverted (33.23), with no count pair**, by the maintainer's call. D147 and D148 go with
  them, and D144 narrows to the streamed types. 33.13.3 stays: it is off the hot path. The uncommitted 33.13.4 rework
  was discarded and 33.22 ticked at the pairs it read and placed.
- **33.13.6–33.13.10 and 33.13.2 leave P33 for P40, sketched** ([`roadmap.md`](../../design/roadmap.md), "P40 —
  Every reader streams", and its inbox). D149, filed for 33.13.9, is struck into that inbox. The output grammars'
  walk 33.13.2 held moves to 33.14, its first consumer, read over a query's held field.

33.23 and 33.19 have landed, 33.20 is struck and 33.14 is split (below); the pickup is 33.14.1.

## 33.20 struck: a census holds a counted container whole

The re-target above sent the gatherer's container tier to P40 and left the census's, which reads the same way: the
count reads a container with `decode_*` (`unrepresentable.rs`, `Counter::tier_of`), the output grammar, whose resumable
walk was 33.13.2 and is P40's. The `*_in` walks a parse streams cannot stand in: they read spellings `decode_*` refuses,
`{ infinity }` in a `date[]` (`nested/output.rs`, "What `decode_*` accepts"), so a census would count a field the query
reading it refuses. Settled with the maintainer: 33.20 is struck, `Kept::Whole` stays, and its field is drawn from the
ledger as a held line is (the spec's "A census holds only the fields it decodes"; P40's inbox). Only a container of a
datetime, `interval` or decimal `numeric` is counted (`Leaf::of`); the spec's `int4range[]` example never was.

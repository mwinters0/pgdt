## The composed `pgdt` does not start in the register's image

Since 30.4, `pgdt` links DataFusion in every build, and with it `libm`
symbols at `GLIBC_2.43` and `GLIBC_2.44` (`atan2f`, `cosh`, …). The register's
image, `measure.Config.image` (`postgres:16`, glibc 2.41), does not hold them,
so a host-built `pgdt` fails to load there for every command, where
`archlinux:base` (glibc 2.44, the host's) starts it:
`runs/30.5-register-image/readings.txt`, on a debug build of the 30.5 tree.

The maintainer settled it: **every figure moves to `archlinux:base`**, the
image `pgdt sql`'s legs already ran in, so the register is one image again and
`pgdt` is built by the host's compiler for its Rust and its C alike. The
image's glibc reaches only what C allocates on the default build, the Rust
heap being mimalloc's, and 30.6 re-takes every figure, so the move breaks no
comparison across sittings. Refused: a toolchain container of `postgres:16`'s
distribution, or a link against an explicit glibc floor from the host
(`cargo zigbuild`), each of which compiles the C dependencies, mimalloc
included, with a second C compiler, and picks a glibc floor that is P29's to
pick; and holding 30.6 until P29 has, which inverts the two phases' order.
P29's inbox carries the consequence for the release build.

The host's glibc will outrun the pinned image again, so a sweep's preflight
starts the built `pgdt` in the image and refuses, naming the pin, when it does
not load. The pin moves only then, deliberately, the next stamp naming its
glibc; refusing on any host-image difference was rejected as making every
`pacman -Syu` an apparatus change. `M215` makes the move and the check, and
blocks 30.6.

## The resident-set instrument leaves the image

`rss_wrapper` (`scripts/measure.py`) is a `perl` program, and `archlinux:base`
holds no `perl` and no `/usr/bin/time` (`runs/M215-image-tools/readings.txt`),
so moving the pin alone leaves every shape reading a resident set unable to
run. The maintainer settled it: the instrument becomes a **static Rust helper**
the harness builds and mounts beside `/pgdt` — still fork, `exec`, wait and
`ru_maxrss` out of `getrusage(RUSAGE_CHILDREN)`, so it needs nothing of any
image and a later pin move cannot break it. It exits with the child's code, or
`128 + signo` for a signal death, writing `signal=<n>` beside `maxrss_kib`;
the cgroup `memory.events` oracle stays, a `SIGKILL` not saying who sent it.
Refused: an image derived from the pin with `perl` at a pinned package version
(a second artifact to build and re-pin, with `pacman` and a mirror in the
apparatus); the host's `perl` mounted in (moved by every `pacman -Syu`,
whatever the pin); cgroup `memory.peak` (charges the dump's page cache to the
container, so another quantity). `M215` carries the helper with the move.

The helper is `peak-rss`, a sixth workspace member, so the per-round checks
build and test it with the rest and its `libc` is the lockfile's; refused, a
Cargo project of its own under `scripts/` (a second lockfile, `check.py` taught
to build it) and a crate-less `rustc` file (a hand-written `struct rusage`, the
per-architecture layout risk `GETRUSAGE_SYSCALL` carries today). It is built
for `<machine>-unknown-linux-musl`, static whatever the host's libc and in a
target dir `target/release/pgdt` does not share; refused, the gnu target with
`+crt-static`, which needs the host's static glibc. `preflight` builds it,
refusing with the `rustup target add` command where the target is missing,
then runs it around `/bin/true` in the pinned image: proof it starts there,
and the floor, reported and never published. At `exec` the kernel folds the
pre-`exec` address space's high-water into the child's `maxrss`, by a reading of
`exec_mmap` not checked against source here, so the floor bounds the spawning
side's share rather than proving it absent; "The instrument" quotes the floor
once a sitting has read it. The shape change stales every resident figure,
which 30.6 re-takes anyway.

## The retention's remedy

The remedy's grilling for mimalloc's retention (30.7) settled three rules,
now in `../../design/roadmap-P30-one-binary.md`, "What the move owes before a
release", and slotted 30.8 ahead of the remedy:

- **The mechanism is confirmed first.** 30.7 names the allocator, not a
  mechanism, and mimalloc's source has two candidates
  (`../../design/roadmap-P30.7-diagnostic-notes.md`, "The candidates in
  mimalloc's source"): `purge_delay` keeping a freed block committed, and a
  cross-thread free waiting on its page's owner. An option fitted without
  that reading would clear the gate on an unnamed cause, the mistake billing
  `KD111`'s unit would make. `MIMALLOC_PURGE_DELAY=0` turns the first off on
  the existing binaries, so the sitting is one variable apart.
- **Re-setting the bound is ruled out** as the remedy: it charges every
  embedder, on any allocator, for a term only `pgdt`'s choice of allocator
  holds, and the bound is P23's (`KD34`).
- **The remedy is read twice**: the gate, and the term gone in every rep of
  both 128 MiB legs. The term showed in four reps of six; three reps all
  missing it is about a 4% chance, six about 0.1%, so the `-m 2g` leg, which
  passes the gate while holding the term, is part of the reading.
- **30.8 is a `reserve` `--alone` sitting**, its legs declared in the register
  entry beside 30.7's, so they differ from 30.7's by one environment variable
  and nothing is subtracted across sittings.
- **An option chosen as the remedy defers to the operator.** In mimalloc
  3.3.2 an option found in the environment is `INITIALIZED`, one not found
  `DEFAULTED`; `mi_option_set_default` writes only the second, from `main`,
  where `mi_option_set` would overwrite the operator's (`options.c`).
- **The remedy's sitting is the whole figure set**: a `purge_delay` of 0
  costs an `madvise` a freed block and a fault-in on its reuse, and a partial
  re-take would leave `measurements.md` timing two builds.
- **A confirmed option is the remedy, registered before 30.8's reading**: a
  time threshold chosen now would have no basis, and weighing option against
  D13 after both readings is the post-hoc call a registered criterion
  prevents. The `allocator` figure stays what would move D13 again.
- **A candidate 30.8 rules out is attributed next**, on the `introspect`
  build, before any remedy: `pgdt` frees blocks cross-thread on every path,
  so the cross-thread list is live (the 30.7 notes, "The candidates in
  mimalloc's source").
- **A library change is a remedy only if it suits glibc as well as
  mimalloc**; one suiting mimalloc alone fits the library to the binary's
  allocator, as re-setting the bound would.
- **A library change suiting both outranks an option**: the option leaves
  upstream's configuration, which is D13's reason for mimalloc, and the
  option's advantage is only its cost. "Suits both" asks nothing of glibc but
  no worse — the term is shipped-only, and glibc already unmaps a freed
  128 MiB block — read from the remedy's full re-take.
- **A partial removal is a confirmation, not noise**: more reps of the same
  legs cannot separate two mechanisms. **30.8 stays one variable apart**: the
  cross-thread instrument (`MI_STAT` 1 in the `introspect` build) is built
  only if a remainder needs it, its counters being exit totals and
  high-waters that cannot say what was held at the peak.
- **D13 reopens only where no library change suiting both allocators and no
  mimalloc option reaches a confirmed mechanism**, never on the option's time
  cost, which the `allocator` figure, D13's evidence, already prices. D13's
  Reopens line, met as written since 30.6.1, now says so.

## What confirms a share

The review of 30.8's two unattended calls settled one amendment and affirmed
one call, before 30.8's readings:

- **A share is confirmed only beyond the control's spread**, by a one-sided
  Fisher exact test at α = 0.05 on the option's and the control's pooled
  counts from the same sitting, registered before the reading. 30.7's control
  legs hold the term in 8 of 12 reps across both builds and both limits
  (`runs/measure-20261005T193210/raw.json`), so an option that does nothing
  reads "fewer reps than the control" in about 41% of sittings and "every rep"
  in under 1%. That makes "fewer", as `measure.confirming_verdict` landed it,
  close to a coin toss on whether `purge_delay` is named as part of the cause.
  At 12 reps a side the test confirms a share where the option holds the term
  in at most 3 reps. "A partial removal is a confirmation, not noise" ("The retention's
  remedy") stands for any removal the sitting can tell apart from the control. The
  test adds no reps, so its "more reps cannot separate two mechanisms" half
  also stands. Anything short of the test reads "not confirmed", and the
  remainder is attributed next. `confirming_verdict` and its tests change
  before 30.8's sitting; that is still slice 30.8, whose checklist line says
  so.
- **A sitting that cannot tell a total removal from the control's spread
  gives no verdict.** One test decides every reading short of "ruled out":
  if gone-in-every-rep would itself fail it against the sitting's own
  control, the sitting confirms and rules out nothing. Otherwise gone in
  every rep confirms the mechanism, and a partial removal is tested as
  above. At 12 reps a side the floor is a control holding the term in at
  least 4; at 2 of 12, an option doing nothing reads all-gone about one
  sitting in nine at p = 0.24. Gating all-gone and reading its failure "not
  confirmed" would hand the next candidate an attribution the sitting never
  made. "Ruled out" needs no test: an option holding the term in every rep
  removed it from none. **A kill on any confirming leg or its control also
  gives no verdict**, naming the leg: the retention line cannot classify a
  killed rep, and dropping it shrinks a count toward confirming. Both change
  `confirming_verdict` before 30.8's sitting, and either is grilled rather
  than re-sat, since 30.7's control held the term in 8 of 12 and killed
  nothing ([30.8's notes](../../design/roadmap-P30.8-confirming-notes.md)).
- **`RESERVE_RETENTION_FRACTION` stays half a unit**, fixed rather than
  measured per sitting, and a shipped rep is still read against its twin's
  worst. The reasoning is in the constant's comment.

## 30.8's read-back goes with it

The maintainer settled, reviewing 30.9's unattended call, that `introspect`'s
`mimalloc_purge_delay` line goes with the rest of 30.8: the line, its
hand-counted `MI_OPTION_PURGE_DELAY`, `purge_delay` and
`the_purge_delay_read_back_is_purge_delay_s` are on 30.9's removal list
(`../STATUS.md`, "P30 progress").

- **Its one reader is 30.8's `purge0` leg**, which 30.9 removes; nothing else
  reads the line.
- **The hand count has no running guard**: `scripts/check.py` runs the suite
  without `--features introspect`, so the test pinning the index runs only by
  hand, and a mimalloc upgrade moving `mi_option_e` would pass the check.
- **P23 is not committed to an option.** The remedy chain ranks a library
  change suiting both allocators above one
  (`../../design/roadmap-P30-one-binary.md`, "What the move owes before a
  release"), unlike 30.7's diagnostic legs, which stay because P23's re-take
  needs their attribution.
- **A general read-back was not built ahead of a phase asking for it**; if P23
  sets an option, how it is read back is P23's call, filed in
  `../../design/roadmap-P23-resident-reserve-inbox.md`.

## `M216`: the cold-NVMe leg is registered; its sitting is next

The harness half landed as admitted (2026-10-05, "`M216`: the cold-NVMe
parallel leg is admitted"). `measure.REGIMES` and `CONTENTION_LIMITS` carry
`cold-nvme-parallel` — the NVMe staging, the cache dropped before every rep,
gated on steal alone as `warm-parallel` is — and `parallel-scan-throughput`
gains a last column, the plain typed provider scan at `PARALLEL_NVME_JOBS`.
Its cache cannot be built in the timed container, as the warm legs' is: the
builder would read the dump back after the drop. `Session.prebuild` writes it
once a sitting in a container of its own and `time_run` mounts it read-only
where `--dump` looks; `PARALLEL_SCAN_PREBUILT` holds why, and the in-container
`fadvise` eviction it rejected. `test_measure.py`'s
`ParallelScanThroughputColdNvme` asserts the leg. A `--reps 1` smoke run,
not publishable, is `runs/M216-smoke/`.

**What remains is the sitting**, which a figure takes from a commit
(`../../design/measurements.md`, "A figure may be published outside the
sweep"): `cd scripts && uv run measure.py --figure parallel-scan-throughput`
from the commit landing this, its table folded in under its marker, and the
"SATA SSD is not a parallel regime" paragraph's last sentence rewritten to the
reading. `M216`'s Date stays empty until then, and its sitting is what `D2`'s
Reopens line waits on.

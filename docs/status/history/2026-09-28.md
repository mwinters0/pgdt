## 27.9's scope, settled with the maintainer

27.8's account (`measurements.md`, "What DataFusion's dynamic filters buy a
query") left 27.9's scope to the maintainer, who settled it:

- **27.9 drops the bounds an `IN` implies from row evaluation and keys each
  group's bounds once in the byte cut** (the spec, "Slices", item 9). Dropping
  the bounds is the duplicate-decoding shape the account says pays best; the
  cut's keying changes no answer and is paid with row evaluation off too.
- **The criterion stays at 27.9, word for word, and off is its expected
  outcome.** By the account, the two removals together fall short of the Δ the
  criterion needs gone at the figures' spreads unless the bounds' removal saves
  near its upper bound. The maintainer accepts off rather than chase the
  criterion: nothing is added to 27.9 to meet it.
- **The lookup stays as it is, and its cheaper shape is `KD55`.** The entry
  weighing it framed the choice as std's SipHash or giving up flood resistance;
  a fast hasher seeded once a process keeps the resistance, and is filed so it
  is not lost.
- **Off brings a switch, in its own slice, 27.10**, taken only if 27.9 turns
  row evaluation off (the spec, "Slices", item 10). Without one the
  unclustered join's win is out of reach, DataFusion's own flag taking group
  pruning and the early stop with it. "D93" refused a switch citing "Two
  tunables", but that rule refuses hardware knobs, not a setting stating
  intent. A separate slice because 27.9 already carries an evaluator change
  and four re-takes.

## `M178`: whether pinning to an L3 group cuts drift, settled with the maintainer

Warm, CPU-bound readings are where drift between sittings lives
(`measurements.md`, "What a session's own drift costs, measured rather than
asserted"), and no figure's container is pinned: `measure.py` states only
`-m`, and `datafusion-cli-pgdump`'s `#[tokio::main]` runtime and the kernel
may place work on any of the 24 CPUs. The 3900X is four L3 groups of three
cores — 0–2/12–14, 3–5/15–17 (die 0), 6–8/18–20, 9–11/21–23 (die 1) — whose
`acpi_cppc/highest_perf` runs 216 on group 0 down to 166 on group 2, with
`prefcore` steering to group 0. The governor was measured and refused
(`measurements.md`, "The apparatus"); affinity never was. The
maintainer accepts coupling the apparatus to this machine to find out.

- **`measure.py --pin-cpus`, off by default**, as `--pin-governor` is. A leg
  of up to 6 threads runs on group 1, one of up to 12 on groups 0+1 (die 0),
  and the harness, its telemetry sampler and whatever else it launches on
  die 1. 16- and 24-worker cells and every leg discovering its count stay
  unpinned, discovery reading the affinity mask (`runtime-invariants.md`, "RT7") and
  their subject being the whole machine.
- **A startup leg**: `datafusion-cli-pgdump` registering the dump and
  answering a trivial query, so each table's fixed cost and its variance sit
  beside the legs it is inside. A longer fixture was refused for it: the
  clustered join's and the TopK's on legs prune to a handful of groups, so a
  bigger file barely lengthens the readings it would be for.
- **The experiment**: `dynamic-filter-join`, `dynamic-filter-topk`,
  `predicate-terms`, `scan-throughput-warm` and one parallel figure, pinned
  and unpinned alternating rep by rep within each sitting, over **three
  sittings**, detached, each gap between them recorded rather than set.
  No interval is required: the only thing a gap could change is what else
  the machine is running, which each reading's telemetry and the contention
  gate already witness (`measurements.md`, "What a session's own drift
  costs, measured rather than asserted"). Compared per arrangement:
  `session-drift`'s median absolute move between sittings, and each leg's
  spread within one. **Pinning is adopted only if its drift is at most half
  the unpinned drift and no leg's spread widens**; otherwise refuted.
- **The clock stays `time`, outside the process**, as a figure must
  (`roadmap.md`, "Attribution is introspective; only the gate is blind"):
  timestamps at `main` and exit were refused, leaving out loading a 200 MB
  PIE, the runtime's 24 worker threads starting (`#[tokio::main]` builds
  it before `main`'s body) and teardown — a trivial `-c "SELECT 1"` reads
  8.9–13.2 ms against `/bin/true`'s 0.4–0.6 ms, sys above real, five warm
  reps by hand in the figures' image, no figure. But it reads to the
  microsecond (`TIMEFORMAT=%6R`, which the image's bash 5.3 honours, where
  `TIME_RE` reads the default's milliseconds), and each reading records
  user and sys beside real.
- **The binaries run from tmpfs, read once untimed before each timed run.**
  `measure.py` mounts them from `target/release/` on the HDD: a first
  trivial run read 1.087 s with `datafusion-cli-pgdump` out of the page
  cache, and every cold regime's `drop_caches` evicts `pgdt` (14.6 MB, 0 B
  resident by `fincore` afterwards) so each cold reading loads it from the
  HDD inside `time`, a term no figure prices and `measurements.md` never
  names. tmpfs is shmem, which `drop_caches` does not evict. Both arms of
  the experiment run staged, and one cold figure is taken staged and
  unstaged alternating rep by rep, pricing the term every published cold
  absolute carries.
- **Adoption is a decision of its own**, an apparatus rule in
  `measurements.md` and a full re-sweep, taken for staging on the priced
  term as for pinning on its drift; the harness option and the sittings
  change none, hence an out-of-band row; no sweep overlaps a sitting.
- **Adoption is validated by re-running the measurements**: the full
  re-sweep, then a second sweep after it, the gap recorded, re-taking
  `session-drift` under the new apparatus against its standing readings —
  the 5.6% median absolute move it plans against among them — so the
  reduction is read off the figure the project already plans against.

## Staging the binaries is adopted, settled with the maintainer

**Every figure, in every regime, runs its binaries staged on tmpfs** — the
maintainer's call: the load is externalized from every reading as far as the
apparatus can, and regimes are normalized against each other, rather than
only the cold ones losing it. Admitted as `M179`, **blocking P27** — also the
maintainer's call: 27.11's figures are taken on the apparatus they are
published under, after `M179`'s two sweeps, rather than taken unstaged and
re-taken by a sweep after P27. Warm medians do not carry the load term, so
this is not a claim that staging moves them. Until it lands the apparatus is
unchanged, and `measurements.md` states what is taken today.

- **It is a bias, not drift.** Loading `pgdt` off the HDD adds the same term
  to every cold rep in one direction; it survives the median, and it does not
  cancel in an against-the-floor ratio, `dd` loading no binary of ours. The
  cold regime's own definition (`measure.py`, `Regime.drops_caches`) is that
  the *input's* bytes come off the disk.
- **The cold-NVMe figures carry it too, at a larger share, unmeasured**: the
  binary sits on the HDD whatever the input's device, and `drop_caches`
  evicts it there as well. If the SSD regime's price carries over, most of
  `scan-throughput-nvme`'s gap to its floor — the ceiling `measurements.md`,
  "Scan throughput by input shape" sizes the I/O-defaults levers against,
  reused by `chunk-size` — is the binary loading. No decision moves: D10's
  refusals only strengthen as that ceiling falls, and `chunk-size` ranks rows
  that all carry the term.
- **`M179` re-takes by a full sweep and then a second on the same commit**,
  which also re-takes `session-drift`, red on `f068c14` and cleared by
  nothing less. Re-taking only the figures that stand alone was refused:
  `scan-throughput-cold`, where the term was priced, is not one of them.
- **`off` and `alternate` stay**, marking a run unpublishable as today:
  `alternate` is what prices the term again, on NVMe included.

# 2026-09-28

## 27.9's scope, settled with the maintainer

27.8's account (`measurements.md`, "What DataFusion's dynamic filters buy a
query") left 27.9's scope to the maintainer, who settled it:

- **27.9 drops the bounds an `IN` implies from row evaluation and keys each
  group's bounds once in the byte cut** (the spec, "Slices", item 9). Dropping
  the bounds is the duplicate-decoding shape the account says pays best; the
  cut's keying changes no answer and is paid with row evaluation off too.
- **The criterion stays at 27.9, word for word, and off is its expected
  outcome.** By the account, the two removals together fall short of the Δ the
  criterion needs gone at the figures' spreads unless the bounds' removal saves
  near its upper bound. The maintainer accepts off rather than chase the
  criterion: nothing is added to 27.9 to meet it.
- **The lookup stays as it is, and its cheaper shape is `KD55`.** The entry
  weighing it framed the choice as std's SipHash or giving up flood resistance;
  a fast hasher seeded once a process keeps the resistance, and is filed so it
  is not lost.
- **Off brings a switch, in its own slice, 27.10**, taken only if 27.9 turns
  row evaluation off (the spec, "Slices", item 10). Without one the
  unclustered join's win is out of reach, DataFusion's own flag taking group
  pruning and the early stop with it. "D93" refused a switch citing "Two
  tunables", but that rule refuses hardware knobs, not a setting stating
  intent. A separate slice because 27.9 already carries an evaluator change
  and four re-takes.

## `M177`: the allocation budget's flake

`the_render_path_allocation_budget_per_row` failed once in 27.8's instrument
round, 2001 allocations against its 2000
(`runs/check/20260927-234454-f6c0a3f6ccd4/nextest.log`), and passed on every
re-run of the same tree. Its counting allocator counted every thread's
allocations while one global flag was set, so the likeliest source is another
thread of the same process allocating inside the counted window — read from the
code, not reproduced. The flag and the count are now thread-locals, as
`tests/statistics_heap.rs`'s already were, and the budget's numbers are
unchanged. The single failure was never reproduced, so its absence afterwards
proves nothing; a recurrence would mean the extra allocation is the test
thread's own.

## The dynamic-filter figures keep pricing row evaluation, settled with the maintainer

27.10 left `measure.py`'s `DYNFILTER_LEGS` varying DataFusion's producer flag
alone, so from `e577576` the "on" leg times the shipped default, rows not
evaluated, and `DFCLI_ACCOUNT`'s recipe introspects a leg evaluating none.
"D93" cites `dynamic-filter-join`'s costing row both as its **Rejected** ("on
by default") and as its **Reopens** ("the costing row's Δ within its legs'
spreads"), and `KD55` is promoted only through that reopening: re-taken as it
stands, that row would read Δ near zero while still cited as the reason rows
are off, which reads as its own reopen condition being met.

- **The figures gain a third leg**: filter off, filter on (the default), and
  filter on with `SET pgdump.dynamic_filter_rows = true` ahead of the SQL, so
  the table prices what ships and what the switch buys and costs. Redefining
  the "on" leg would leave the shipped default unpriced; timing the default
  alone would leave "D93" citing a reading no command reproduces.
- **"D93" reads its reopening against the default leg, not filter off**:
  it reopens when, on the costing row, the rows leg lies within the default
  leg's spreads *and*, on the unclustered row, beats it with the spreads
  apart — 27.9's two-part criterion with the default leg in filter off's
  place, and its **Rejected** cites the same comparison. The switch decides
  rows on against rows off with the producer on, and only the `SET`
  separates those two legs; filter off mixed the producer's cost into the Δ.
- **`dynamic-filter-topk` takes the same three legs**, from the one
  `DYNFILTER_LEGS`: the setting covers a TopK too, and 27.10's stop asked of
  every kept row in an armed group is priced only against the rows leg.
- **`DFCLI_ACCOUNT`'s recipe profiles and introspects the default leg
  against the rows leg**, the comparison "D93" reopens on; 27.8's filed
  account keeps its own sitting and pair.
- **This is slice 27.11, ahead of P27's wrap** (the spec, "Slices", item 11):
  it restates "D93"'s decision, so it is no out-of-band row. The third leg,
  `test_measure.py`'s assertions, "D93" rewritten and both figures re-taken
  land there; until then "D93" and the apparatus describe what is built.

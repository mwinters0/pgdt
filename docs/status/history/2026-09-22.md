# 2026-09-22

## The session budget

Reviewed with the maintainer after 6.5 landed; 6.5.1 built what it changed
([`../STATUS.md`](../STATUS.md), "P6 progress").

- **A scan draws at plan time and holds the draw for its plan's life**, as 6.5
  built it; drawing at `execute` is rejected in `ScanBudget`'s rustdoc.
- **A late scan gets the remainder**, and the join shape that starves is
  `KD38`, unowned until a measured join shows the cost.
- **DataFusion's memory pool was left out of the allowance.** Its default is
  unbounded, but `datafusion-cli --memory-limit N` gives its operators N bytes
  (`MemoryPool::memory_limit` answering `Finite(N)`, 55.1.0), which the
  provider's scans could also draw. The spec's "Workers and memory" now takes
  a finite pool limit off the allowance, as 6.5.1 built it.
- **The no-limit carving differs from `pgdt`'s by design**, recorded on
  `ScanBudget::discover`.
- **The consistency scripts did not know the provider crate.** `KD38`'s marker
  is its first in `datafusion-pgdump/src`, and `deficiencies.py` scanned only
  `pgdump_query` and `pgdt`, so its `CODE_ROOTS` gained the crate here.
  `citations.py` and `repoint.py` gained it at `M129`, which moved the meter
  by the crate's comments; each script's tests assert its roots against
  `Cargo.toml`'s workspace members.

## The resident map

Reviewed with the maintainer after 6.5 landed. The map stays resident for a
registered dump's life, and a cache rebuilt after open waits for a reopen —
both on `PgDump`'s rustdoc. **Nothing billed that residency**: `pgdt`'s
`map_file` charges loaded statistics as `Term::Loaded`, the provider charged
nothing, and N registered dumps held N maps under `MEMORY_RESERVE` alone. The
spec's "Workers and memory" now takes each dump's loaded statistics off the
allowance, and 6.5.1 carries it beside the pool limit.

## Which bound the holdings come off

Reviewed with the maintainer after 6.5.1 landed. 6.5.1 takes the pool limit
and the resident statistics off both of `Parallelism::within_shared`'s bounds;
they come off the margin's ceiling alone, which 6.5.2 built.

- **`pgdt` already bills statistics this way** (D85, `statistics_allowance`),
  `Term::Loaded` included, and is safe because `MEMORY_UNPOOLED_BOUND` bounds
  a scan's excess over its budget. The cap's larger `MEMORY_RESERVE` is the
  scans' own, and neither a map nor a pool limit is part of it.
- **Where a scan's count can fall, the two rules differ only where the cap
  binds**, below `5 × (MEMORY_RESERVE − MEMORY_UNPOOLED_BOUND)`. Where it
  cannot — a source recommending no per-reader memory, whose count
  `Parallelism::fit` never bounds, and a count already at one worker — the
  holdings now leave the budget alone, where 6.5.1 lowered it
  ([`../../design/roadmap-P6.5.2-holdings-margin-notes.md`](../../design/roadmap-P6.5.2-holdings-margin-notes.md)).
  Flagged in `../STATUS.md`, "Decisions worth another look".
- **Taking the holdings off the allowance instead is rejected**: the margin,
  a fraction of the smaller number, would let predicted resident pass it by
  that fraction of the holdings. Recorded on `ScanBudget`'s rustdoc.

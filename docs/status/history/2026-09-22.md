## The session budget

Reviewed with the maintainer after 6.5 landed; 6.5.1 built what it changed
([`../STATUS.md`](../STATUS.md), "P6 progress").

- **A scan draws at plan time and holds the draw for its plan's life**, as 6.5
  built it; drawing at `execute` is rejected in `ScanBudget`'s rustdoc.
- **A late scan gets the remainder**, and the join shape that starves is
  `KD38`, unowned until a measured join shows the cost.
- **A finite DataFusion pool limit comes off the allowance** (spec, "Workers
  and memory"; 6.5.1): `datafusion-cli --memory-limit N` gives its operators N
  bytes (`MemoryPool::memory_limit` answering `Finite(N)`, 55.1.0).
- **The no-limit carving differs from `pgdt`'s by design**, recorded on
  `ScanBudget::discover`.
- **The consistency scripts read the provider crate**: `deficiencies.py` for
  `KD38`'s marker, `citations.py` and `repoint.py` at `M129`, each script's
  tests asserting its roots against `Cargo.toml`'s workspace members.

## Which bound the holdings come off

Reviewed with the maintainer after 6.5.1 landed. 6.5.1 takes the pool limit
and the resident statistics off both of `Parallelism::within_shared`'s bounds;
they come off the margin's ceiling alone, which 6.5.2 built.

- **`pgdt` already bills statistics this way** (D85, `statistics_allowance`),
  `Term::Loaded` included, and is safe because `MEMORY_UNPOOLED_BOUND` bounds
  a scan's excess over its budget. The cap's larger `MEMORY_RESERVE` is the
  scans' own, and neither a map nor a pool limit is part of it.
- **Where a scan's count can fall, the two rules differ only where the cap
  binds**, below `5 × (MEMORY_RESERVE − MEMORY_UNPOOLED_BOUND)`. Where it
  cannot — a source recommending no per-reader memory, whose count
  `Parallelism::fit` never bounds, and a count already at one worker — the
  holdings now leave the budget alone, where 6.5.1 lowered it
  ([`../../design/roadmap-P6.5.2-holdings-margin-notes.md`](../../design/roadmap-P6.5.2-holdings-margin-notes.md)).
- **Taking the holdings off the allowance instead is rejected**: the margin,
  a fraction of the smaller number, would let predicted resident pass it by
  that fraction of the holdings. Recorded on `ScanBudget`'s rustdoc.

## The holdings at the floors

Reviewed with the maintainer after 6.5.2 landed. Where a scan's count cannot
fall, 6.5.2 leaves its budget alone however much the session holds, so the
predicted resident can pass the margin, and the allowance, by one reader's
cost a scan. The margin holds there too: 6.5.3 builds it, and `M130` extends
it to `pgdt`.

- **Two floors.** `Parallelism::fit` returns `DEFAULT_MEMORY_BUDGET.min(cap)`
  for a source recommending no per-reader memory without reading the ceiling,
  and `affords` never goes below one reader, whose `at(1)` on a compressed
  source is a whole-block decode.
- **The rule**: holdings come off the ceiling, and whatever of them the
  ceiling's room cannot absorb at the resolved count comes off the budget, for
  every source. A budget pushed below a unit declines it (D4), a compressed
  scan losing block decode. The cap stays the reserve's. Rejected: 6.5.1's
  rule, which bills the holdings against the reserve's room where the cap
  binds; and a plain-source exception, what a plain budget is worth being
  `KD25`'s and `KD32`'s.
- **`pgdt` has the same gap for a loaded cache.** `map_file` bills
  `Term::Loaded` to the `StatisticsAccount`, which bounds only further
  gathering (D85), and the workers are carved before it
  (`statistics_allowance` reads the carved budget), so `Parallelism::within`
  passes `held = 0`. `M130` passes the loaded statistics as `held`, D85's
  order changing with it; it waits on 6.5.3's rule and blocks nothing in P6.
- **The provider's new exposure was the pool limit**, which `pgdt` has no
  analogue of and which can be gigabytes; the maps already matched `pgdt`.

## A `macaddr` literal in Arrow semantics

Reviewed with the maintainer after 6.6 landed, which left every `macaddr` term
to DataFusion. The defect is the library's: Arrow semantics keeps
`CompareKind::MacAddr` (`arrow_order`) and reads its literal in either case of
hex (D55), so `v = '08:00:2B:01:02:03'` matches `08:00:2b:01:02:03` where
DataFusion, comparing the `Utf8View`, does not. The spec already says the
`Utf8View` kinds compare bytewise ("Comparison means what DataFusion means");
6.2 shipped short of it, so the fix is **slice 6.2.2**, blocking nothing.

- **`MacAddr` moves to `Text` under `arrow_order`**, answering every literal
  as DataFusion does. Rejected: a lowercase-only literal, refusing the rest;
  and a per-literal switch keeping the octet key for a canonical one, the
  only such switch in `resolve_term`, to save range pruning.
- **It announces `ValueAsText`**, whose doc says a `macaddr`'s reaches only a
  literal not in the server's spelling, its order over the file's text
  agreeing. Every variant still affects ordering (`v < 'A0:…'`).
- **The oracle asserts that every kind emitted `Utf8View` or
  `Dictionary<Utf8>` is `Text` under `arrow_order`.** Its walk draws literals
  only from what the server wrote, which is why it never saw this.
- **What it costs is Arrow range pruning on `macaddr`**: `bounds_ordered_in`
  goes false, the dictionary keeping `=`. Gathering is unchanged, so no
  `CACHE_FORMAT_VERSION` bump (D78). 6.9 lands after it, and whether its
  bytewise gathering covers every kind Arrow compares as text — `macaddr`, a
  bare `numeric`, `timetz`, `inet`/`cidr`, `jsonb` — is its question.

## Which columns carry bounds in Arrow's order

Reviewed with the maintainer after 6.9 landed
([`../../design/roadmap-P6.9-bytewise-text-bounds-notes.md`](../../design/roadmap-P6.9-bytewise-text-bounds-notes.md)).

- **6.9 re-takes no figure.** `statistics-gathering` stays under P23's hold
  (`roadmap.md`, "P23 — Statistics coverage and the resident reserve", "The
  figures owed"); 6.9's notes say how the figure should move.
- **The rule is every semantics whose order over the file's text is exact**,
  one set where the orders coincide: the spec's "Comparison means what
  DataFusion means" says so. It is 6.7's input as much as pruning's — 6.7's
  `Exact` `MIN`/`MAX` is defined over Arrow's order — so under 6.9's rule alone
  a value-ordered column is `Absent` in DataFusion for the cache's shape's sake.
  Rejected: 6.9's rule; one slot filled by the first exact semantics, which
  leaves every kind whose PostgreSQL bounds are not Arrow's without Arrow
  bounds — an enum, `interval`, a bare `numeric`, `timetz`, `inet`/`cidr`,
  `character(n)` under `C` (`ARROW_AGREEMENT`'s bounds column, `predicate.rs`).
- **It lands as slice 6.10, after 6.9 and before 6.7**, a cache-shape change
  kept out of 6.9's tested one: `jsonb`, `character(n)` off `C` and a column
  with no plan bounded in Arrow's order, and a second set keyed by
  `arrow_order()` for those kinds, bumping `CACHE_FORMAT_VERSION` (D78).
- **`macaddr` is the one moved kind whose stored set is Arrow's**: over
  `macaddr_out`'s fixed-width lowercase hex (I40) octet order is byte order,
  so `bounds_ordered_in(Arrow)` means "these stored bounds are Arrow's".
  Rejected: a second set, the same strings twice; leaving it unbounded.

## A column no DDL declared is bounded, its set chosen by what gathering stored

Reviewed with the maintainer after 6.10 landed, which bounded only a
*declared* column with no plan
([`../../design/roadmap-P6.10-arrow-order-bounds-notes.md`](../../design/roadmap-P6.10-arrow-order-bounds-notes.md),
"Negative results").

- **The spec stands**: a column with no plan, a `NotDeclared` one included, is
  bounded in Arrow's order. 6.10's narrowing is its shipped contract, and the
  remainder is the earned **6.10.1**, ahead of 6.7, whose `Exact` bounds read
  the set it chooses.
- **The hazard 6.10 named is in how a set is chosen, not in keeping it**: a
  term chose it from the query's plan, where under `SchemaMode::Strings` every
  column is `Refused` while gathering resolved it `Typed`. The discriminant is
  stored: `ColumnStatistics::declared_type`, `None` where nothing declared it.
- **So a set is read by the kind gathering stored it under**, recomputed from
  its declared type and collation against the metadata as gathering resolves
  it, and matched against the kind the term compares by — `macaddr`'s octet
  order counting as its text's. A data-only column reads its `Text` set; a
  Strings-mode `integer` reads none; a Strings-mode text column gains its
  `Text` set. Rejected: `ResolvedSchema` recording whether DDL was consulted,
  which marks the ambiguity rather than removing it; each set storing its
  kind, which the declared type and the register already determine.

## A statistic answers where reading the column refuses

6.7 amended its verification item: over a column holding a value its Arrow
type cannot represent (`KD8`), `COUNT(<column>)`, `MIN` and `MAX` taken off the
map answer where a read refuses. Affirmed with the maintainer; reasoning and
rejected alternative in D89, and what a null resolution of `KD8` would break at
its anchor.

- **`COUNT(<column>)` is what the fixtures expose.** `COUNT(*)` decodes no
  column, and a bound that fails to decode makes both its column's bounds
  `Inexact` (`ColumnSummary::finish`), `date`, `timestamp` and typed
  `numeric` specials ranking at the extremes (D56). An overflowing `interval`
  is no extreme in `IntervalFields` order and exposes `MIN`/`MAX`; no fixture
  gains one, its value having no oracle and `decode_interval`'s tests pinning
  the overflow.
- **`M131`** checks the carved-out `COUNT`s against a Strings-mode read, which
  never raises `KD8`, and runs the target in both modes.

## The binary's sink reports at planning, and a scan's counts are metrics

Reviewed with the maintainer after 6.8 landed, which prints nothing a query
raised (its notes, "Negative results"). The spec's "Diagnostics: one sink" is
amended, and **6.8.1** builds it ahead of the wrap.

- **A `PlanNote` is a `Finding`**, its severity `pgdt`'s per-kind choice moved
  into the library, drained to the table's sink from `scan()`, where
  `TablePartitions::plan` settles it; `pgdt` reads it back. The warnings this
  reaches (`ParallelismBudgetLimited`, `CompressedBlockPathDeclined`,
  `AllocationBelowFloor`) never printed in the binary. Rejected: owning the
  REPL for "after the statement"; printing beside the sink.
- **Groups pruned and bytes an early stop left unread are `PgDumpExec`
  metrics** under `EXPLAIN ANALYZE`, as Parquet's pruning is. Rejected: `Info`
  drained per partition, which the stderr sink never prints.
- **Attaching a dump folds `UnknownCollation` into one line** in `StderrSink`
  (`M132`); the provider still reports per column. That warning is false on a
  `--create` or `pg_dumpall` dump, whose `CREATE DATABASE` states the
  collation and is left `Unparsed`: `KD40`. Reading it changes the
  register's call, so it is grilled, not admitted out-of-band.

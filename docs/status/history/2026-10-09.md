until they are decided:

  verified under qemu-user, by the maintainer's call; real arm64 ran the suite
  in CI's `arm64-suite` job. The call binds any later release on any
  architecture, and is filed nowhere: it wants a line in
  `../../design/decisions.md`, "D104", or in `../../design/roadmap.md`,
  "Standing rules".
- **`/release`'s step 4 says this host runs no arm64 code**
  (`.claude/skills/release/SKILL.md`), which stopped being true when qemu-user
  and its `binfmt` rule were installed here. `release_ci.py smoke` takes no
  `--platform`, so v0.1.0's arm64 leg called `step_smoke` with one added by
  hand. Whether the rehearsal smokes arm64 is a change to the skill.

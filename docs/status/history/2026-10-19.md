## The `Dump`'s table owns its blocks; `info` reads the cache

Two of 36.8's unattended calls, reviewed with the maintainer.

**`pgdt info` stays off the `Dump`** (`docs/design/decisions.md`, "D61"). It
reads the `LoadedCache`'s index, size, container shape and envelope, reports a
partial cache and, through `OfflineCache`, one with no source; a `Dump` holds
only a complete map and offers tables, which `info` never reads, listing per
block. What the spec moved it for, the open protocol written out by hand, went
with 36.6's `CacheMode::open`. The spec's "The opened dump" and slice 8 now say
`pgdt query` alone opens through both layers.

**A `Table` owns what it plans over**, reversing the borrow 36.8 landed: it
shares the source, the watch and the DDL by `Arc` and holds its own blocks.
`Dump::table` builds one, and the early-stopped cold pass yields one for the
table it settled, in place of `MappedTable` — one type both passes hand back,
`'static`, so `PgDumpTable` holds it and `table_of` goes. The borrow's case
was cost, and it was the handle's alone: `table_schema` and
`TablePartitions::new` clone the table's blocks on every call, a plan the whole
`DumpMetadata` too (`crates/pgdt-query/src/stream/table.rs`,
`stream/partitions.rs`), so a table holding its blocks clones once where the
adapter clones per use today. A cold metadata-level table's query-only census
(D35) lives in that table's own blocks. Where the DDL's `Arc` sits, in
`DumpIndex` or beside it in the `Dump`, is the implementing slice's.
**36.8.3 builds it, ahead of 36.8.1**: a self-contained reshape of the module
36.8 landed and of `PgDumpTable`, kept apart from 36.8.1's rework of the
tested stream entry points, which then builds the type rather than designing
it.

Refused: keeping the borrow and giving the early-stopped pass a second type
with the same four methods; a `Table<'a>` borrowing a settled map rather than
a `Dump`, with a second public owner lending it — one type more, two steps to
a table.

## `pgdt query` bills the statistics its replay holds

Reviewed with the maintainer: 36.8.4's call that a `pgdt query` holds its
table's statistics through a replay carved around none (`KD123`). Admitted
as `M227`, rather than left for a reading to promote: the defect is the
memory gate's own. `M227` has landed; `KD123` is struck.

**The fix is `pgdt`'s**: the replay is carved after `Table::settle`, around
the settled table's statistics heap, as the provider bills its resident map
(`docs/design/decisions.md`, "D85"). Both holders keep them by `Arc` — the
plan for `TablePartitions::under`, and the `Table` `pgdt query` keeps alive
through the stream — while `ReplayPlan` holds none, its pruning distilled into
`kept` and `stops` at plan time.

**What `query` prints**: `resolved the arrangement` still names the replay,
announced after the mapping pass once it is carved, with `held_bytes=` where the
settled table holds statistics. The mapping span keeps its `held_bytes` and
loses `replay_jobs`, a count not yet announced when the pass runs, and the
manual's account of both (`docs/manual/dump-inspection.md`) changes with it.
Refused: announcing the replay before the pass and again where its bill lowers
it, the first line naming a count that may not run; announcing the mapping
pass's carve as that line, printed where the pass maps nothing.

As landed: `Discovered::resolve_pass` carves either pass around what it
holds (`crates/pgdt/src/parallel.rs`), the settled table's heap summed over
`Table::blocks` in `pgdt` rather than added to `pgdt-query`'s API. A query
whose pass finds no table prints no `resolved the arrangement`, no replay
being carved. Pinned by `parallel/tests.rs` and `status_output.rs`'s
`a_query_s_passes_say_what_they_were_carved_around`.

Refused: a plan keeping no statistics until a dynamic filter is handed to it.
It reshapes the library's plan and the adapter's call for a saving the
provider never sees, its `Dump` billed whole, and frees nothing alone, the
`Table` still holding them.

## `IN` is an operator

Reviewed with the maintainer: 36.8.2's call to keep `IN` as `Expr::In` once
each operator carries its value. Reversed, as slice **36.13**, taken after
`M227`, which now blocks P36.

**A membership is a term.** It tests one column against literal operands
and holds no `Expr`. The tree already reads it as a leaf
(`resolve_loosened`, `first_unrepresentable_test`), and PostgreSQL parses
`x IN (consts…)` as one `ScalarArrayOpExpr`, `=` under `ANY` over an array,
`NOT IN` as `<> ALL` (`transformAExprIn`, `src/backend/parser/parse_expr.c`).
Its `Or` fallback is for list items holding columns, which D53 refuses. That
it answers as the `Or` of `=` is its semantics, as `IS DISTINCT FROM`'s is a
composite of its own; its `NULL` is its operand's domain. One kind of leaf
for an embedder to build outweighs the branch that moves from `Expr` into
resolution, the resolved membership staying its own form.

**The shape**: `PredicateOp::In` and `NotIn`, each holding
`Vec<Option<String>>`, `NotIn` because every test carries its negation as a
variant and an embedder translating `InList { negated }` or `<> ALL` then has
a direct target; it answers as `In`'s negated truth, and only `In` lets
`for_rows` drop the bounds it implies. `value()` gives way to one accessor
naming the operand — none, one literal, or a set — because `None` or the
first value would misstate a list. `pgdt`'s grammar keeps `TermOp::In` beside
`TermOp::Compare`: the distinction is a parenthesised list against one value,
and both arms then build a `Predicate`. D53 is rewritten when 36.13 lands.

**Placed after 36.8.2**: it reshapes the filter surface that slice just
touched, and the adapter's translation (`filter/pushdown.rs`,
`filter/dynamic.rs`) before 36.11 rebuilds the adapter, which would
otherwise rebuild it twice.

## The column record is its finding

Reviewed with the maintainer: 36.9's call that `ResolvedColumn` holds its
`ColumnNote` whole. Reversed. **The record is the column's finding**, and
`ColumnNote` goes; the spec's "Values passed back together" says so. That
work is **36.9.1**, next in the checklist so that 36.11's public `Located` and
36.12's embedder test build on the final finding type. `ResolvedSchema::notes()`
goes with it, an alias for `columns()` once the record is the finding, and
`drain` takes `resolved.columns()`. **36.9.1 is counted**: the spec lists
rendering through the column record as a hot-path reshape, and the slice moves
that record's layout, which `render_field`, `render_field_into` and
`decode_field` read per field.

The spec's sentence ("the copy of each column's resolution in its note goes")
was ambiguous as written, the record also being said to hold the note: a note
stripped of its resolution cannot state its own severity or message. 36.9 took
the copy beside the note. What it left: the name stored twice, in the field
and in `note.column`, equal only because both constructors (`resolve_columns`,
`column_of`) copy one into the other. `ColumnNote` is built nowhere but inside
a record, no production sink downcasts to it (only the two test sinks,
`pgdt-query/tests/diagnostics.rs` and `datafusion-pgdump/tests/registration.rs`),
and `DiagnosticSink::report` takes `&dyn Finding` by borrow, so the record
itself can be handed to a sink: it holds every input to `severity` and
`message`.

Refused: affirming 36.9 (one fact stored twice, kept equal by construction);
a `ColumnNote` kept as a transparent view over the record, a reference cast
buying only the old downcast target's name, at the price of a type holding
nothing.

## A rise reached through changed code is the change's

Reviewed with the maintainer: 36.9's acceptance of the data-level `parse`
rise as the change's, without reopening how `--count-pair` is read. Affirmed,
and the reading written into the spec's "What a hot-path slice owes" before
36.9.1, the next slice counted.

What decides it (`runs/attr-36.9-20261019-callgrind/reading.txt`): the rise
is `memcpy` under `ColumnGatherer::close_group`, identical code called as
often, but the frame that moved it belongs to `Gatherer::observe_row`, which
the reading classes `changed: size differs` — machine code the tree changed,
no line of its source. 36.4.3's "layout" was a rise with no changed code behind
it, the instrument pricing something other than the change; this one is
reached through changed code. The analyzer's class totals file the whole rise
under `OUTSIDE PGDT`, so the caller chain, not the self charge, is what a
reading of identical code reads.

Refused: reading the rise as layout by 36.4.3's letter and redesigning the
instrument, which 36.1.1's identical-source pair showed does not price the
build; reworking until `observe_row`'s frame returns to `0x278`, a stack offset
no source shape addresses; a materiality floor below which a resolved rise
owes no attribution, a threshold with no account behind it, and one that
would have passed this rise unseen.

## A refusal a front end cannot spell is reshaped until it can

Reviewed with the maintainer: 36.10's call that two clauses keep the library's
words, `pgdt` unable to see into the variant's `String` they ride in, and that
`pgdt sql` spells nothing. Reversed on all three; the work is **36.10.1**,
next in the checklist, and 36.11's row.

**Under the strings schema mode an ordering operator is refused with a
remedy**, not an explanation: ask it in the typed schema mode, which `pgdt`
spells `--schema-mode typed`, raised where the mode is known as
`UnrepresentableTestUntyped` is, by `query_tests` at plan time
(`crates/pgdt-query/src/stream/replay/plan.rs`). **One variant answers both**,
that one generalised to a test the strings schema mode cannot answer, its
sentence naming what the operator needs: same cause, same remedy, the same
place raised, so an embedder would act on two variants identically. The aside
D107 rejected spelling was never strings mode's alone: `NOT_MAPPED` fires for
every resolution but `Mapped` and `UnrepresentableValues`
(`crates/pgdt-query/src/predicate/notes.rs`, `compares_as_declared`), so a
typed-mode column of unknown type read it too, and predicate resolution never
sees the mode, strings mode reaching it only as `NotDeclared`.

**An enum's accepted form cut short says so as a fact**: `PredicateValueDecode`
carries how many labels it left unlisted, and `pgdt` spells the place they are
listed, `pgdt info --detail`. Where the rest of the labels are written is a
pointer the user acts on, so a remedy by D107's own test; listing every label
was refused, nothing bounding how many a type declares.

**`pgdt sql`'s shell spells in its own grammar** (`--dump …:strings`,
`:strict-identity=TERMS`), and hands that spelling to the adapter at
construction, beside the sink 36.11 already moves there. Upstream's `exec`
prints a statement's error under `-f` and the REPL
(`crates/datafusion-cli-pgdump/src/lib.rs`, `KD122`'s marker), out of reach
of anything in `run()`, so a refusal must carry its spelling in its message
before it crosses DataFusion's boundary, which every one crosses at one
conversion (`crates/datafusion-pgdump/src/error.rs`). Refused: spelling in
`run()` alone, which reaches registration and `-c` and leaves the same
refusal bare in the REPL.

**The adapter names no front end's command either.** `CacheNotComplete` and
`MetadataLevel` (`crates/datafusion-pgdump/src/error.rs`) write `pgdt parse`
into their sentences today, in the crate an embedder other than `pgdt sql`
uses. In 36.11 both state the mapping pass as the library does and carry what
a spelling needs as data — the invalid-values mode they were opened under, so
`KD92`'s fix survives as a fact, and the database where it matters — their
`parse` strings going; the shell spells `pgdt parse …`.

**A log line is a library sentence too.** The back-fill's `info` event on a
block whose statistics were declined names `--memory`
(`crates/pgdt-mapping/src/backfill.rs`), read by any embedder subscribing to
`tracing` and beyond any spelling layer's reach; 36.10.1 says it as a larger
statistics allowance, `allowance_bytes` already carrying the number, and the
manual's quotation of it (`docs/manual/dump-inspection.md`) changes with it.

**36.10.1 lands before 36.11**, so the shell's
exhaustive spelling is written once, over the variants 36.10.1 settles.

D107 describes what is built until 36.10.1 lands, which rewrites it.

## A misspelled column is refused before what it asks

Reviewed with the maintainer: 36.10.1's call that `query_tests`
(`crates/pgdt-query/src/stream/replay/plan.rs`) raises `TestNeedsDeclaredType`

## The aggregate's filter is off whatever the config says

`ready_session` turns `enable_aggregate_dynamic_filter_pushdown` off whatever
the builder's config holds, where P36's spec had it off "unless the caller
already set that option": DataFusion 55.1's `ConfigOptions` holds a plain
`bool` (`datafusion/common/src/config.rs:1528` at 55.1.0) and no record of
whether it was stated. The maintainer affirmed the call; the spec now says
what is built, and the rejected alternatives are UF1's Workaround
([`../upstream.md`](../upstream.md), "UF1").

In 55.1 `SET datafusion.optimizer.enable_dynamic_filter_pushdown` sets all
four filter options, the aggregate's included (`config.rs:2024–2035`), while
`RESET` of it restores only the TopK's and the join's (`config.rs:1979–1987`);
`RESET` of the aggregate's own option restores DataFusion's default, on. The
manual said the umbrella's `RESET` turned it on; it now says what 55.1 does
([`pgdt-sql.md`](../../manual/pgdt-sql.md), "Types"). Holding those three
cases in `pgdt sql`'s test is admitted as `M228`, which has landed:
`the_aggregate_dynamic_filter_is_off_unless_stated`
(`crates/pgdt/tests/sql.rs`) holds each, the umbrella's `RESET` both from the
shell's off and after the option's own `SET`.

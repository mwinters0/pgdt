## The arm64 suite is built where it runs

The arm64 suite was cross-built in the x86-64 image as a nextest archive,
uploaded as an artifact and run on the arm64 runner. A rehearsal here wrote that
archive at 2.4 GB (`runs/29.4-workflows/suite-archive-j6.log`), which every
release would have uploaded and downloaded; the archive is not in git and was
not kept. The maintainer decided the arm64 runner builds and runs the suite
itself: `release.py suite`, as the x86-64 job runs it, with no suite artifact
and no `suite-archive` or `suite --archived` step. The suite is a job of its own
(`arm64-suite`, needing only `preflight`) beside the smoke run's
(`arm64-smoke`, needing the archive), so the cold test build does not wait on
the cross release build. The spec's "The build image"
says so; the mechanism and what 29.6 inherits are
`../../design/roadmap-P29.4.1-arm64-suite-notes.md`.

The doctest decision `M220` left to the first library holding one is gone: the
arm64 suite runs the doctests, so `test_release_ci`'s test that failed on one is
deleted.

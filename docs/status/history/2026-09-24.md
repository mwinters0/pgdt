# 2026-09-24

## P25 is struck; P26 and P27 are filed

P25 hands DataFusion what the map's statistics already say, or can say from
the parse that gathers them. It was grilled, sliced, landed, wrapped and struck
at its keystone today: its decisions are `decisions.md`'s "D89" and "D91"
(with "D51" and "D79"), and the two misses its notes recorded are `KD46` and
`KD47`. Its grilling filed two phases, both sketched in
[`../../design/roadmap.md`](../../design/roadmap.md):

- **P26 — statistics refused on their cost, reconsidered**: the bloom filter
  and distinct-count sketch P25 left out, and every refusal the register
  grounds in gathering cost.
- **P27 — DataFusion's dynamic filters**: needs no new statistic, but moves
  pruning into the replay's stream; "D53" is reconsidered there.

`KD39` split in the grilling: its text-`MIN` half was struck by 25.5, as was
`KD42`; its enum half is `KD45`, owned by P27, whose dynamic filters meet the
same label order.

**Slice 25.7 was admitted after 25.6 landed**, from the review of 25.6's
text byte sizes kept only for a typed `Utf8View` or `Binary`: that left a
`:strings` scan and a typed scan's booleans and enums with no total, so
`join_selection` fell back to rows, and closing it cost one `u64` a group per
column, the count already being made. It landed in P25 rather than at a later
bump, none being scheduled. The reasoning is "D91"'s.

## Repoint findings for the maintainer

The wrap's repoint (stamp `b87bd10` → `15ed465`) corrected what was phase-local;
these are what it may not edit unattended — the register, a standing rule,
`measurements.md` — or where the code, not the record, falls short. Each is a
blind reader's, with both lines named; those marked † were not re-opened.

**How they are disposed of**, settled with the maintainer:

- Where the code is right and the record or a comment is wrong, the record is
  corrected to the code without a question, each correction listed; one goes
  to the maintainer only where it is unclear which side states the intent.
  These land as one "record corrected" commit, no `M<k>`.
- Where the code is wrong, it is fixed wherever the intent is already written
  (a `D<k>`, a comment, PostgreSQL's behaviour) and the fix fits a session; a
  `KD<k>` only where closing it needs a decision nobody has made.
- Fixes land as one `M<k>` per module cluster — predicate/pgtype, the
  provider, gather/statistics accounting — from `M142`, a fix needing a
  decision split into its own item.
- **`SET search_path` is not read, and stays unread for now**: an unqualified
  name resolves as under the default path, and where a declared object could
  shadow it under a path pgdt does not read, the answer is the weaker one
  (unknown, not bytewise), as `KD4`'s is. Not reading it becomes a `KD<k>`,
  (c) unowned, its fix being to model the path — allocated by `M142`.
- **A domain cycle is bounded, and `KD4` is closed beside it**: a depth bound
  on the three recursive resolvers returns `Unknown` (`M142`), since an ignored
  rename makes a cycle whatever the spelling; normalizing a quoted and an
  unquoted name alike on both sides of a lookup closes `KD4` and the
  case-mismatch route (`M143`).
- **D85 is corrected to the code**: a block passing the allowance before its
  last group closes declines; the close `finish` makes is kept, bounded by one
  charge step, and the next block declines at its first charge. Record only.
- **`EVERY_OUTCOME` is tied to the enum by a `macro_rules!` in the test**,
  listing the variants once for both the list and a wildcard-free match, the
  test asserting the list is `EVERY_OUTCOME` plus the written exemption; no
  dependency. The roadmap's fixture rule names the exemption (`M144`).
- **The `gather.rs` maximum test asserts only what the code has**: a level
  whose last group is unpaired is exempt, and `KD43`'s example is pinned as a
  test the marker cites, so closing `KD43` flips it (`M144`).
- **The provider builds every table at catalog construction**, from the map
  alone: a refused table is left out of `table_names` and kept as a finding
  `report` sends with its reason, so `SHOW TABLES` lists what can be queried
  and no lazy build — nor its race — remains. Beside it, `summary.rs` checks a
  column's stored groups against the block's, and `SHOW ALL`'s
  `pgdump.memory` text says the session budget's own allowance (`M145`).
- **A query's mapping pass says what lowered its count**, `bound_by=` as the
  leader's shortfall does; and a signal after the mapping pass's last check
  or during the listing exits 128+signal, as D26 states (`M146`).
- **Order**: one "record corrected" commit (every (R) finding, D85's included,
  `measurements.md` via `measure.py`), then the items in allocation order;
  only `M142` before `M143` matters, both reworking the same resolvers.

## M310's remainder, reviewed

- **The maintainer affirmed that the kill licence stays in the session** (`measurement/timing.py`, `KILL_TOLERANT`,
  whose comment says why): a kill's meaning is the session's concept, the family only its data.
- **`parse-cache-out`'s `/out` is one contract spelled in two files**: `shapes._script` writes under it and
  `Session.time_run` mounts the warm directory there, matched by nothing. It is a shape's, run by three figures, so
  `shapes` takes it (`M311`). A mount table across every mount was refused: the instrument's is keyed on
  `RunSpec.instrument`, not a shape, and `PREBUILT_MOUNT` is another container's.
- **`gzip-resident` declares no stand-in, so its dry run fails at its renderer**, which refuses a report without
  `resolved_jobs`, the mirror's `resolved_budget` and, on a first-pass leg, `first_pass_workers`
  (`figures/gzip_resident.py`). Nothing dry-runs every figure; `M312` gives it the stand-in and the suite that test.

## M311: a shape states where it writes its cache out

- `shapes.CACHE_OUT_MOUNT` is the in-container directory and `shapes.CACHE_OUT_SHAPES` the shapes writing there;
  `_script` writes `parse-cache-out`'s cache under the first and `Session.time_run` mounts the warm directory there
  for the second. `test_shapes.py` holds that a shape writes under the mount exactly when it is named, and
  `test_timing.py` that `time_run` mounts it for those shapes alone.
- `timing.PREBUILT_MOUNT` keeps its own spelling of `/out`: it is `Session.prebuild`'s container, not a shape's.

## 15.7.1's writers and its bar, reviewed

With the maintainer, closing the two "Decisions worth another look" entries
15.7.1's instrument round filed. `gzip-first-pass` is landed untaken; these
amend it before its readings, as the slice's own remaining work.

- **The zlib-ng level-6 leg is Fedora 44's `zlib-ng-compat`
  (`2.3.3-3.fc44`), pinned by that version.** The PyPI `zlib-ng` package it
  was written through bundles zlib-ng 2.2.5, which writes a different stream
  at level 6, 25 blocks to Fedora's 27 and 4% larger on one payload
  (`runs/p15.7.1-writers/reading.txt`). So the leg was not the `pg_dump -Z`
  its label names. The level-1 leg stays on the package. Its single static
  block is `deflate_quick`'s, and Fedora's build writes dynamic blocks at
  level 1. The stock zlib and GNU gzip legs stand. GNU gzip ends blocks about
  half as often, the one level-6 shape that differs. pigz, igzip and other
  levels stay out: the spec's producers are `pg_dump -Z` and `pg_dump | gzip`.
- **A mixed order settles too.** Once the physical count beats the logical
  count, the physical count beating the logical count less one reads as a
  shared core, capped at the physical cores (which also removes the
  oversubscription), and otherwise as oversubscription. As landed, *12 > 23 >
  24* and *23 > 12 > 24* fell through to `undecided`, and 23 workers and the
  scan still fill every SMT sibling, so both costs showing is a likely reading.
- **"Beats" needs a margin as well as the signs**: six of seven paired wins
  *and* a median paired difference over 3% of the faster leg's median. "Level"
  is anything else. Signs alone let a sub-percent gap move the default, and
  wrote "level", a failure to show a difference, into D2 as a rejection. 3% is
  about half the gap 15.17.4's pairs found between twelve and twenty-four jobs
  (`runs/p15.17.4/gate/`). Seven reps stay.
- **A file the pass slows is named, not passed over.** `no lift` splits:
  `regresses` where serial beats any upper count by the same bar, named in the
  verdict's text, a `KD<k>` filed by the round that reads it. It does not
  block the default, the spec's question being which upper count, and a
  regression being a defect in speculation that finds nothing. The level-1
  leg is the likely one: its first pass is the frontier's alone
  ([`../../design/roadmap-P15.10-static-notes.md`](../../design/roadmap-P15.10-static-notes.md),
  "What later slices inherit") while the other workers speculate.

## `KD147`, reviewed

With the maintainer, closing the "Decisions worth another look" entry 15.7.1's
readings filed. `KD147` is owned by a P15 slice, 15.19, ahead of 15.7.2
([`../../design/roadmap-P15-gzip.md`](../../design/roadmap-P15-gzip.md),
"Slice order").

- **The producer is real, though you have to opt in to it twice.** Arch's
  `extra/zlib-ng-compat` 2.3.3 provides `libz.so=1` and conflicts with `zlib`.
  It is built from the same package as the `zlib-ng` whose level 1 writes one
  static block (`runs/p15-level1-writers/reading.txt`), so `pg_dump -Z1`
  linked against it writes this file. The default count slowing such a file
  past serial is a defect in the shipped default, whatever it costs to fix.
- **The fix waits on no attribution.** Every candidate cause — siblings, caches,
  the clock, the frontier's handling of failed guesses — goes away once
  futile speculations are not handed out. The profile chooses no lever; it is
  the slice's opening account.
- **The lever is the frontier's block starts**, not a run of empty
  speculations, which needs a constant, nor recognising the file and reading
  it serially. It is inert where blocks are short, and resumes at the next
  block or member.
- **The serial pass's cost on this file earns no `KD` yet.** It is
  unattributed. `native/walk.rs`'s `Walk::run` decodes every symbol of a
  static block from the block's start, so over a one-block member it walks
  the whole member beside the engine, and that makes D118's walk the leading
  candidate. 15.19's profile settles it. A walk it names reopens D118 for the
  maintainer.

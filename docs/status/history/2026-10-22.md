# 2026-10-22

## 15.1.1: the inflate figure is built, untaken

- **15.1.1 is the serial inflate figure alone**, and is unticked: its
  instrument, `inflate-throughput`, is registered in `measure.UNTAKEN`
  ([`../../design/roadmap-P15.1.1-inflate-notes.md`](../../design/roadmap-P15.1.1-inflate-notes.md)),
  and its readings and its zlib-ng leg remain. The readings are taken from the
  commit carrying it, `cd scripts && uv run measure.py --figure
  inflate-throughput`.
- **The candidate spacings are 15.1.2**, earned by the same split 15.1's was
  (`../../process.md`, "Slice numbering"). A spacing leg times a checkpoint
  taken during a forward inflate or an inflate resumed from one, which no
  backend's stable API offers — zlib's `Z_BLOCK`, `inflatePrime` and
  `inflateSetDictionary` sit behind `zlib-rs`'s unstable `__internal-api`
  and `libz-sys`'s C ABI, `miniz_oxide`'s behind its `block-boundary`
  feature (the spec's "Facts the grilling found") — so its instrument is a
  checkpoint-and-resume implementation of its own, the core of what 15.4
  builds in `gz-seek`, and a review apart from a serial inflate's.
- **zlib-ng has no binding that builds in the release image**; the attempts
  and their failures are the notes doc's "Negative results", and the call
  is STATUS's "Decisions worth another look".

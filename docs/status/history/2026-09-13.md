## `M104`: a sweep that lost a figure still wrote a full session stamp

`emit` chose between a session stamp and a partial-sitting header on
`whole_sweep`, computed **before the run** as "did the selection cover
`FIGURES`". A run that selected all 22 and took 20 therefore emitted
"**All 23 figures below come from that sitting.**" over 20 tables, three
paragraphs below a blockquote naming the two that failed. Pasting it would have
asserted something untrue of two tables that stay in the document from an older
sitting — and the stamp speaks for those too, which is the whole reason a
partial sitting does not re-stamp.

`whole_sweep` is now `selected_sweep and not failures`, decided **after** the
figure loop. That means the marker each table carries cannot be decided inside
the loop either — a figure taken third cannot know the nineteenth will fail — so
the loop collects `(id, heading, tail)` and the markers are filled in once, when
the predicate is known. A degraded sweep says so in the log, and its lead counts
the tables it actually emitted rather than the figures it selected.

`--render` gets the same correction from the other side: it iterated
`raw["figures"]`, which is the *selection*, so replaying a sitting that lost a
figure would have died on a missing reading key rather than rebuilding the
tables it did take. It now drops the ids in `raw["failures"]` and re-derives
`whole_sweep` against them, so an older `raw.json` carrying the pre-fix value
cannot restore a stamp its run was not entitled to.

## A plain `parse` is faster than serial warm, which reverses the record

## The two open decisions are closed: the plain serial default stands

**A plain source keeps recommending one worker, and the reason is that the scan
is device-bound rather than that splitting it is slow.** The warm re-take —
1.36× at two workers, 1.41× at four — retired the old justification and does not
replace it: the leg is warm on tmpfs, the regime that exists to expose CPU a
device hides, while on storage the scan sits at 1.00× the SATA floor and 1.06×
the NVMe's. Two things decide it beyond that, and neither was in the entry that
raised it. Splitting a plain source **adds** device bytes: `PLAIN_PARTITION_CHUNKS`
makes each worker read a chunk-sized tail past its own 8 MiB partition, 12.5%
more by construction, against an NVMe budget that is the 0.072 s by which a
1.289 s scan exceeds `dd`. And no figure covers a parallel plain scan on a real
device — both `parallel-*` figures are `warm-parallel` only, and
[`../../design/measurements.md`](../../design/measurements.md), "Scan throughput
by input shape" rejects the cold-parallel legs by arithmetic, noting that
scattering reads is fatal on rotational media. The decision is filed beside the
mechanism as a rejected alternative
([`../../design/decisions.md`](../../design/decisions.md), "I/O, memory and
parallelism"), and what reopens it is now narrower than what P19

## `M263`: `pgdt sql` takes `--source`

- **`pgdt sql`'s `--dump` is `--source`**, as `parse`, `query` and `info` name a dump; admitted and landed at the
  maintainer's word. Its value is unchanged, `[NAME=]SOURCE[:…]`, its `SOURCE` what the other commands' `--source`
  takes. Nothing recorded why the two differed: `--dump` came with `datafusion-cli-pgdump` (6.8), whose `Args`
  `pgdt sql` mounts, and DataFusion's CLI names no `--source`.
- **Figures timing the shell (`dfcli_invocation`'s argv) now spell `--source`**; nothing read changes, the flag
  registering the same catalog.

## `M264`, `M265`: a cache is a `.pgdt` file, named by `--cache`

- **The default cache is `<dump>.pgdt`** (`pgdt-dump`'s `cache::store::colocated_path`), and a URL's
  `./<last segment>.pgdt`; **the flag naming one is `--cache`**, as `--dtcache` was, `none` included. Both admitted and
  landed at the maintainer's word.
- **A cache left under the old name is not found by default, and `--dtcache` is refused as unknown.** Neither has a
  fallback, this being pre-1.0. A cache renamed to `.pgdt` is read as before, recording its source's identity and
  nothing of its own path.

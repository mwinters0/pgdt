# 2026-09-23

## Which keys a budget-quoting plan note's clause names

Reviewed with the maintainer after 6.12 landed, which named `pgdump.memory`
always and the chunk and partition keys only where the library's sentence
offered them (`BudgetedPlanNote::clause`). The spec's "Diagnostics: one sink" is
amended.

- **A key is named only where it would change what the note reports**, for
  this source and this budget; a key that cannot is not offered. A fixed map of
  every key is rejected as telling the reader nothing about the note.
- **On a plain source one reader costs `max(chunk, min(8 × chunk, 8 MiB))`**
  (`Partitioning::partition_bytes`) **and the budget is `DEFAULT_MEMORY_BUDGET`
  capped by the carved allowance** (D83, `KD32`), so `AllocationBelowFloor`
  there has three cases: a zero budget (an allowance at or under
  `MEMORY_RESERVE`), where only memory moves it; a budget between zero and one
  reader, where memory and the chunk both do; and a budget at the cap under a
  stated chunk past it, where only the chunk does and 6.12's clause offered
  only `pgdump.memory`. The same cap makes `pgdump.memory` inert under
  `ParallelismBudgetLimited` and `BatchSpanNarrowed` on a plain source, whose
  own sentences say a larger budget is not a larger allowance there.
- **The library's `AllocationBelowFloor` sentence names its levers**, as its
  two siblings do: a larger memory budget always, with their caveat where the
  source recommends no per-reader cost, and a smaller read chunk only where the
  source cuts by the chunk and the budget is above zero. `pgdt`'s `--chunk-size`
  readers get it too.
- **The library exposes the levers as data**, not only in its sentence: a
  plan note says which of a larger allowance, a smaller read chunk and fewer
  sub-streams would move it, computed where the budget is carved and the
  reader sized, and the provider maps each to its key —
  `datafusion.runtime.memory_limit` beside the allowance where the pool states
  a limit. The provider re-deriving D83's cap and the reader's shape is
  rejected as a second copy of both.
- **6.12 shipped the wrong contract, so the fix is the earned 6.12.1**, one
  slice for the library's sentence, its levers and the provider's clause,
  ahead of the phase wrap.

## Which keys a budget-quoting plan note's clause names

Reviewed with the maintainer after 6.12 landed, which named `pgdump.memory`
always and the chunk and partition keys only where the library's sentence
offered them (`BudgetedPlanNote::clause`). The spec's "Diagnostics: one sink" is
amended.

- **A key is named only where it would change what the note reports**, for
  this source and this budget; a key that cannot is not offered. A fixed map of
  every key is rejected as telling the reader nothing about the note.
- **On a plain source one reader costs `max(chunk, min(8 × chunk, 8 MiB))`**
  (`Partitioning::partition_bytes`) **and the budget is `DEFAULT_MEMORY_BUDGET`
  capped by the carved allowance** (D83, `KD32`), so `AllocationBelowFloor`
  there has three cases: a zero budget (an allowance at or under
  `MEMORY_RESERVE`), where only memory moves it; a budget between zero and one
  reader, where memory and the chunk both do; and a budget at the cap under a
  stated chunk past it, where only the chunk does and 6.12's clause offered
  only `pgdump.memory`. The same cap makes `pgdump.memory` inert under
  `ParallelismBudgetLimited` and `BatchSpanNarrowed` on a plain source, whose
  own sentences say a larger budget is not a larger allowance there.
- **The library's `AllocationBelowFloor` sentence names its levers**, as its
  two siblings do: a larger memory budget always, with their caveat where the
  source recommends no per-reader cost, and a smaller read chunk only where the
  source cuts by the chunk and the budget is above zero. `pgdt`'s `--chunk-size`
  readers get it too.
- **The library exposes the levers as data**, not only in its sentence: a
  plan note says which of a larger allowance, a smaller read chunk and fewer
  sub-streams would move it, computed where the budget is carved and the
  reader sized, and the provider maps each to its key —
  `datafusion.runtime.memory_limit` beside the allowance where the pool states
  a limit. The provider re-deriving D83's cap and the reader's shape is
  rejected as a second copy of both.
- **6.12 shipped the wrong contract, so the fix is the earned 6.12.1**, one
  slice for the library's sentence, its levers and the provider's clause,
  ahead of the phase wrap.

## A narrowed batch span names the read chunk

Reviewed with the maintainer after 6.12.1 landed, which listed no chunk lever
on `BatchSpanNarrowed`. The spec's "Diagnostics: one sink" rule — a key named
where it would change what the note reports — already requires one; `M134`
brings the code to it, ahead of the phase wrap.

- **A smaller chunk widens a narrowed span on a chunk-sized source**:
  `derived_source_span` divides `budget − charge.at(jobs)` among the readers,
  and a plain reader's charge is a multiple of the chunk. A plain source at a
  64 MiB budget, four sub-streams and the default chunk narrows to 8 MiB with
  every reader seated, so only this note fires, and at a 256 KiB chunk the
  span is 14 MiB. The allowance being inert there (D83, `KD32`), the provider
  offered only `datafusion.execution.target_partitions`.
- **The lever is listed wherever `Partitioning::sized_by_read_chunk` holds**,
  with no above-zero condition, a zero budget never reaching this note alone.
- **The sentence names it in `AllocationBelowFloor`'s clause, gated on the
  lever, with no caveat**: `ParallelismBudgetLimited`'s "seats rather than
  speed" exists because a seat may not become throughput (`KD17`), and a
  wider batch is itself what the caller stated.
- **The checks** are `pgdump_query/src/stream.rs`'s
  `a_plan_note_lists_only_the_levers_that_move_it`, which also shows the
  span widening under the smaller chunk, `datafusion-pgdump/tests/scan_reports.rs`'s
  `a_narrowed_span_on_a_plain_source_names_the_chunk`, and
  `pgdt/tests/parallelism.rs`'s narrowed-span test. No figure was taken.

## `pgdt query`'s two passes are carved apart

Reviewed with the maintainer after `M130` billed a cache's whole statistics
against both of `query`'s passes. Admitted as `M135`; landed unattended.

- **The library already takes one arrangement per pass**: the mapping pass
  reads `ScanOptions::parallelism`, the replay `QueryOptions::parallelism`
  (its partition cut and its `hint_parallelism`), and nothing requires them
  equal. `pgdt` filling both from one carving was the whole of the coupling,
  so no library carving is needed.
- **Each pass is billed what it holds.** The mapping pass holds the loaded
  index while its readers extend the map, and is billed the whole statistics
  as `held`. Over a map that already settles the table it reads nothing
  (`map_forward`'s early returns), so the whole holding meets no reader. The
  two passes never run at once, so each is carved from the one allowance with
  nothing `drawn`.
- **The replay holds no loaded statistics, made true rather than billed.**
  A `Segment`'s copy of its `CopyBlock` was the one `Arc` keeping a matched
  block's statistics alive once the index was dropped, though nothing reads
  them after `ReplayPlan::new` settles the kept runs and stops.
  `Segment::new` drops them, the serial `table_stream` drops its matches
  before replaying, and `pgdt` carves the replay with nothing held
  (`Discovered::resolve_query`).
  Billing the matched blocks instead is rejected: they are known only after
  the mapping pass, so the library would carve, and it is handed an
  arrangement rather than an allowance.
- **Bringing them back is local**: a replay that wants a block's statistics
  keeps the `Arc` its `CopyBlock` already carries, and bills it in the
  replay's carving. P21, which gathers during the replay, is where that may
  be wanted.
- **`resolved the arrangement` announces the replay's carving**, the one a
  plan note's origin clause quotes and the only one that reads over a settled
  map; the mapping pass's shows on its own `scan started` line, printed only
  when it reads. The manual's "`scan started`'s `jobs=` is the count `resolved
  the arrangement` announced" carries `query`'s exception.
- **P21's inbox holds the premise**
  ([`../../design/roadmap-P21-query-statistics-inbox.md`](../../design/roadmap-P21-query-statistics-inbox.md)):
  a P21 gather that keeps or merges into a replay's statistics breaks it
  silently, under-billing with nothing failing. P23's sketch and P22's inbox,
  which still called a query's billing an unspent branch, now state it taken.
- **One item, both halves together**: the `pgdt` half alone would carve the
  replay at nothing held while `Segment` still holds the matched blocks'
  statistics. It blocks nothing, P6's remaining slices being the provider's,
  which never passes through `pgdt query`'s carving.
- **The checks** are `pgdump_query/src/stream.rs`'s
  `a_replay_holds_no_block_s_statistics` and `pgdt`'s
  `a_query_s_replay_is_carved_around_no_statistics`. No figure was taken.

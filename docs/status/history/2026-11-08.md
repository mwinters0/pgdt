# 2026-11-08

## 33.13.3 split at the order key's readers: 33.13.4 earned

33.13.3 asked every scalar reader to read a byte source forward. Its readers are two populations: a `TextGrammar`'s
(`bit_in`, `varbit_in`, the seven geometric inputs, a nested `box[]`), which only a strict parse reads, and those an order
key is read by (`field_key`: the integers, `oid`, the floats, `numeric`, the datetimes, `interval`, the network types,
`macaddr`, `uuid`, `bool`, an enum), which the statistics gatherer reads on every data-level parse
(`statistics/gather/bounds.rs`) and a filter on every query. Bundled, a review accepts a strict-only change and a rework
of a tested core path at one confidence (`docs/process.md`, "Size a slice by its review, not by its scope").

- **33.13.3** is rewritten to the first population and landed
  ([`roadmap-P33.13.3-grammar-notes.md`](../../design/roadmap-P33.13.3-grammar-notes.md)); its allocation test reads a
  long `polygon[]` and `bit varying[]` element.
- **33.13.4** takes the second, a range's bounds and a scalar column's escaped field, and the zero-padded `numeric[]`
  element of the row's allocation test. It inherits `Strtod` (`value/libc.rs`), a `strtod` with no read-ahead a
  `float8in` reader can take.

## 33.13.4 split at the readers whose state is fixed: 33.13.5 earned

33.13.4 asked every reader an order key is read by to read a byte source forward. Five hold a fixed state and replace a
slice reader of a few dozen lines each — the integers', `oid`'s, `boolean`'s, an enum's and `uuid`'s; the rest are
`float*in` over `Strtod`, `numeric_in` with its typmod rounding and its v14 and v16 spellings, `ParseDateTime` and the
date and interval decoders over `datetime/grammar.rs`, `network_in` and `macaddr_in`'s `sscanf`, and a range's bounds —
each a review of its own, too many for one session's (`docs/process.md`, "Slice numbering").

- **33.13.4** is rewritten to the five and landed
  ([`roadmap-P33.13.4-key-notes.md`](../../design/roadmap-P33.13.4-key-notes.md)); its allocation test reads a
  zero-padded `bigint[]` element.
- **33.13.5** takes the rest, a range's bounds and the zero-padded `numeric[]` element of the row's allocation test.


## 33.13.5 split at `numeric`: 33.13.6 earned

33.13.5 held the six readers 33.13.4's split left, each named there a review of its own. `numeric_in`'s is one: its
grammar, both majors' spellings and the typmod's rounding, the one reader whose answer — a bare column's key — grows
with its value, so it holds digits rather than a fixed state (`decisions.md`, "D148").

- **33.13.5** is rewritten to `numeric` and landed
  ([`roadmap-P33.13.5-numeric-notes.md`](../../design/roadmap-P33.13.5-numeric-notes.md)), with the row's zero-padded
  `numeric[]` allocation test.
- **33.13.6** takes the rest — the floats over `Strtod`, the datetimes and `interval` over `datetime/grammar.rs`,
  `network_in`, `macaddr_in` and a range's bounds — with an allocation test of its own.

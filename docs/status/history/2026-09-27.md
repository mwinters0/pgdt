## `statistics_never_change_an_answer` fails under load, from 27.3 on

`datafusion-pgdump/tests/statistics.rs` passes alone and fails when the
machine is busy: `SELECT MIN("v_small") FROM t_numeric` refuses in one session
(`KD8`, the `NaN` in `id` 3's row) and answers `-1.50` in the other. The table
is one row group cut into two partitions, and whether a scan reaches the `NaN`
before the aggregate's dynamic filter, tightened by the other partition's
`-1.50`, rules the group out depends on which partition runs first — the
refusal `D54` lets pruning give up, now decided by timing. Twelve copies of the
test binary at once, three times over, fail at `9af196d` and on 27.4's tree
alike: `runs/statistics-flake-20260927/`, one log a run. The decision it
needs is [`../STATUS.md`](../STATUS.md), "Decisions worth another look".

# 2026-09-16

## A plain `query`'s parallelism is bounded by the batch pin, not by the budget

Reviewing 20.6's first "Decisions worth another look" entry established where
a plain `query`'s one-piece arrangement actually comes from, which the entry
stated but did not follow. The call the entry raised — a stated `--memory`
leaving a plain dump on `DEFAULT_MEMORY_BUDGET`, exactly as a discovered limit
always did — **stands**, and `decisions.md`, "D83" is unchanged.

**The binding term is `QueryOptions::max_source_span`**, a caller-side batch
pin rather than the source's cost, whose default equals `DEFAULT_MEMORY_BUDGET`
by coincidence of two lineages — now `KD32`.

**The option the entry's binary excluded** — neither pricing a plain reader
(`KD25`) nor handing a zero-cost source the whole cap (`D3`) — is to derive the
span, which [`../../design/roadmap.md`](../../design/roadmap.md), "Two tunables
fit pgdq to hardware: memory and parallelism" already prescribes and which
`plan_partitions` needs no new plumbing and no third flag to do.

## What 20.6 did to the `parallel-*` legs

Settled by arithmetic against the carving, no run taken. `PARALLEL_LEGS`
(`scripts/measure.py`) is four legs, and the stated
`--memory stated_allowance(PARALLEL_BUDGET)` reaches them differently now that
`Parallelism::within` carves a typed number.

- **Plain, `parse`** — 64 MiB budget from `fit`'s zero-cost arm against an
  8 MiB footprint: eight readers. `PARALLEL_JOBS` runs to 24, so its 12, 16 and
  24 rows now resolve the same eight workers as its 8 row.
- **The two `.xz` legs** — a real `WorkerMemory`, so `margin_allowance` binds
  where it did not before: at a 2432 MiB allowance the ceiling is 1,689 MiB
  against a 2,048 MiB cap. **Their resolved count is unchanged** — 29 readers
  afforded instead of 35, both past the axis's top of 24 — but the *budget*
  they announce falls from the stated 2,048 MiB to `memory.at(24)`, 1,369 MiB,
  because the old path passed `fit`'s count through while keeping the typed
  number as the budget. That budget is what clamps the pool's slots (`KD21`),
  so it is `parallel-peak-rss`, not the throughput table, that moves on this
  leg; by how much is 20.9's to read.

## `M111`: a plain `query`'s batch span is derived, and `--jobs` buys readers again

The rule and its floor are
[`../../design/decisions.md`](../../design/decisions.md), "D84"; the mechanism
is `plan_partitions` and `derived_source_span` in `pgdump_query/src/stream.rs`.
`QueryOptions::max_source_span` is now a ceiling that `table_stream_partitions`
spends against the budget before it cuts the count, and the value it settles on
is written back onto the sub-streams' own options, so the charge the plan was
solved against is the size the batches actually flush at.

**What a plain `query` now resolves**, at `DEFAULT_MEMORY_BUDGET` against an
8 MiB reader, is the whole axis pinned by
`stream::tests::a_stated_span_is_spent_down_to_seat_the_readers_and_no_further`:
one sub-stream at `--jobs 1`, rising to seven from `--jobs 8` up, where the span
is on its floor and the count is what gives. The shortfall past seven is a
`ParallelismBudgetLimited` as before; the narrowing itself is a new
`PlanNoteKind::BatchSpanNarrowed`, and the two legs of
`pgdump_query-cli/tests/statistics.rs` that 20.6 left planning one sub-stream
plan three again, each asserting the count off that note.

**`QUERY_SUBSTREAM_CAP` is `{"control": 7}`** (`scripts/measure.py`), still
hand-computed on its own argument; the `.xz` legs are untouched, a
partition-retaining source never being charged the span. Every `parallel-*`
figure is stale on that path, which 20.9 re-takes.

## The floor reviewed: right shape, wrong unit

Grilling `M111`'s two entries. `MIN_SOURCE_SPAN` stands *as an argument* and is
wrong *as a constant*.

**The floor is one retained unit, and a larger one is refused** — filed as
`decisions.md`, "D84"'s `Rejected` and `Reopens` lines.

**The unit is the shipped chunk where it should be the announced one.** D84's
own wording is "one read chunk"; the constant is `SCAN_CHUNK_DEFAULT_SIZE_BYTES`, and
`pgdq query --chunk-size` reaches every value the manual documents as measured.
Wrong in both directions: at a larger chunk the span is narrowed below what a
batch pins anyway (`RetainedChunks` pins whole chunks), and at a smaller one
the floor holds it *above* the room the budget had and costs sub-streams — at
`--chunk-size 64k --jobs 64` the plan seats 42 readers where the announced unit
seats the 64 asked for, then blames the budget in a `ParallelismBudgetLimited`
note. The plumbing the entry called missing is present —
`table_stream_partitions` holds the `scan_options.chunk_size` that `replay`
announces — so the fix is a sixth argument. Admitted as `M112`.

**Checked and not a hole**: the charge adds the span `S` where a batch pins
`⌈S/chunk⌉ + 1` chunks, but that under-bill is dominated by `KD25`'s over-bill
up to a 1 MiB chunk and above it is `KD24`'s written promotion condition.

## The narrowing note reviewed: kept, and its advice corrected

**Announcing every narrowing stands** — filed as `decisions.md`, "D19"'s
`Rejected` line, beside the statistics skip stated at zero, the same call. The
alternative suppresses the note across `--jobs 2`–`7`, where the count is
delivered in full and this is the only signal, and keeps it from `--jobs 8` up,
where `ParallelismBudgetLimited` already names the span.

**Its remedy clause is inert where it fires.** The note fires only on a
chunk-retaining source, which `Parallelism::fit`'s zero-cost arm holds at
`DEFAULT_MEMORY_BUDGET.min(cap)`, so "raise the memory budget" does nothing at
or above 64 MiB — `KD32` as user-facing text; and a note quoting a budget
should carry `plan_note_origin`, appended by severity today, which held only
while every budget-quoting note was a `warning:`. Both are `M114`.

## P20 wraps without setting the reserve, and the line is put down

The attribution sitting completed — readings in
`runs/20.8-reserve-attribution-20260916-1857/readings.json` — and **the constant
is not set from them**: P21 and P22 both move what it answers to, so fitting it
now prices a model about to be replaced. They keep, as P23's input.

**The phase delivered its bound and not its coverage**, the discovery that ended
it. `Term::Retained` is never released during a pass, so a long dump fills its
allowance partway through and every block after declines: statistics are a
prefix of the file (`KD33`), the reserve does not cover what a run holds
(`KD34`), and P23 owns both.

Negative results are [the phase
notes](../../design/roadmap-P20-statistics-memory-notes.md). Four figures
stay red until the constant settles; a repoint is owed before P21 and P22.
